## Insights

1. **"B1" is the anchor term, not "Deutschtest".** The b1-prüfung family (208k) is about 20× the
   deutschtest family (10k). "deutschtest" itself has only 480/month.
2. **German beats English about 8:1** in DE (208k vs 27k), which supports German as the default
   language. English demand in DE skews to A1 (spouse visa, A1 letter writing).
3. **telc B1 is big** (telc b1 prüfung 8,100 plus variants). Many people likely call the DTZ
   "telc B1", because telc ran it before g.a.s.t. **TODO(hannes):** verify, and decide whether
   telc Deutsch B1 is in scope (it needs its own blueprint).
4. **DTZ core is real and winnable:** dtz prüfung b1 4,400 (KD 30), a2 b1 deutschtest für
   zuwanderer 390 (KD 20), deutschtest für zuwanderer prüfungstraining 260 (KD 13).
5. **KD is mostly 20–40**, which a new site with deep content can win. **CPC is low**
   (€0.30–0.85), so paid search is cheap, and advertisers aren't fighting over these terms.
6. **Skill subgroups are large** (mündliche 534/767, schreiben 437), which backs `/dtz/sprechen`
   and `/dtz/schreiben` as standalone pages.
7. Noise to exclude: `polizei` (police entry test), `realschule`/`klasse` (school), `termine`/`vhs`
   (booking), `pdf`.
