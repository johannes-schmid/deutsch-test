## 1. Variants: what shares this format

| Exam | Same format as ZD? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Zertifikat Deutsch** | **Yes, same exam.** "Zertifikat Deutsch (telc Deutsch B1)" is the official double name. | [S1][s1] p. 2, 5 |
| **telc Deutsch B1 Schule** (Zertifikat Deutsch für Jugendliche) | **Same skeleton** with the same item counts, timings and 300 points. The differences: instructions use *du*, the topics suit teenagers, and the Sprechen parts are renamed (Kontaktaufnahme · Gespräch über ein Thema · Gemeinsam eine Aufgabe lösen). The oral can be "Paar- oder Einzelprüfung". Writing criterion I is called "Berücksichtigung der Leitpunkte". The engine can be shared; the content and copy cannot. | [S4][s4] p. 5, [S5][s5] |
| **telc Deutsch B1 Beruf** | **No exam by that exact name found.** The sibling is **telc Deutsch B1+ Beruf**. ## 2. Merge verdict

**Verdict: telc B1 is a separate test. Merge it with neither the DTZ nor Goethe B1.** The sections, item types, timings, scoring and result logic all differ (table below). Shared parts are limited to the engine: R/F items, a/b/c items, matching, an email task and a pair-speaking simulator.

| | **telc Deutsch B1 (ZD)** [S1][s1] | **DTZ A2·B1** [S8][s8] | **Goethe-Zertifikat B1** [S9][s9] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Result | Pass/fail at B1 (60% written **and** 60% oral) + grade | Scaled: B1, A2 or below A2 (CLAUDE.md §2; DTZ Übungstest has a section "Zertifikat A2 oder Zertifikat B1?") | Per module: pass at 60/100, with Prädikat |
| Modular? | No. One written block + one oral, but a failed part can be retaken alone (§4) | No (one sitting) | **Yes**: 4 modules, taken alone or combined |
| Order | Lesen+SB → Hören → Schreiben | Hören → Lesen → Schreiben | Lesen → Hören → Schreiben (15-min breaks between) |
| Lesen | 3 Teile, 20 items, **plus a Sprachbausteine subtest** (2 Teile, 20 items); 90 min for both | 5 Teile, 25 items, 45 min; Teil 5 is a 6-gap letter (no separate SB subtest) | 5 Teile, 65 min |
| Hören | 3 Teile, 20 items, **all Richtig/Falsch**, ~30 min | 4 Teile, 20 items (a/b/c, R/F, matching), 25 min | 4 Teile, ~40 min |
| Schreiben | **1 email** (informal or semi-formal), 4 Leitpunkte, 30 min | 1 letter (semi-formal/formal), 30 min | **3 tasks**, 60 min |
| Sprechen | Pair, ~15 min + **20 min prep**: kennenlernen · Thema (contrasting opinions) · planen | Pair, ~16 min: über sich sprechen · Bild/Erfahrungen + Prüferfragen · planen | Pair ~15 min (single 10 min) + **15 min prep**: planen · Präsentation · Feedback/questions |
| Points | 300 (written 225 + oral 75) | (scaled per skill) | 100 per module |

## 3. Sections

Lengths are measured from one official mock (Übungstest 1 [S1][s1]) unless stated otherwise. They show the typical size, **not a specification**. Audio lengths come from transcript word counts at ~140 words/min, cross-checked by silence detection on the official mock audio [S3][s3].

### 3a. Leseverstehen (90 min together with Sprachbausteine)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Globalverstehen | Match a headline to each short text | 5 short press items/notices (studies, consumer tips, travel offers, book tips) | ~65 words per text (5 texts ≈ 340 words) + 10 headlines a–j | **5 · matching from 10 headlines** (each used once, 5 left over) | – | shared 90 min | [S1][s1] p. 5–7 |
| 2 Detailverstehen | Read one article, answer a/b/c | Press/magazine article (social or work topic) | ~320 words | **5 · a/b/c** | – | shared | [S1][s1] p. 5, 8–9 |
| 3 Selektives Verstehen | Find the ad that fits each situation | 10 short situations (1–2 sentences) + 12 small ads a–l (restaurants, travel, tutoring, courses, clubs) | Ads ~15–50 words each | **10 · matching from 12 ads + "x"** (no fitting ad). Each ad used once. 2 of 10 were "x" in the mock. | – | shared | [S1][s1] p. 5, 10–11, 41 |

**Instruction style (paraphrased pattern):** read the headlines/situations and the texts, then find the matching one for each. Each option may be used only once. In Teil 3, mark x if no ad fits. Mark the answers on the answer sheet (Antwortbogen S30). Instructions use *Sie*.

### 3b. Sprachbausteine (inside the 90 min)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Grammatik | Fill gaps in a letter, choose a/b/c | Personal letter/email (in the mock: informal, to a friend) | ~150–170 words | **10 gaps · a/b/c** (options test conjunctions, articles/case, auxiliaries, adjective endings, prepositions, relative pronouns, pronouns) | – | shared | [S1][s1] p. 5, 12 |
| 2 Lexik | Fill gaps from a word pool | Semi-formal/formal letter (in the mock: an inquiry replying to an ad) | ~170 words | **10 gaps · matching from 15 words a–o** (each fits once; 5 distractors) | – | shared | [S1][s1] p. 5, 13 |

**Instruction style:** read the text and close the gaps. Teil 1: which option (a, b or c) is right. Teil 2: use the words a–o, each fits only once.

### 3c. Hörverstehen (~30 min)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Globalverstehen | Decide if a statement matches each speaker's gist/attitude | Radio vox pop: a short intro, then 5 people give their view on one everyday question (mixed male/female voices) | ~45–90 words per statement (≈20–40 s) | **5 · Richtig/Falsch** (1 per speaker) | **once** | 30 s reading time before | [S1][s1] p. 14, 42; [S3][s3] |
| 2 Detailverstehen | Follow one conversation in detail | Radio interview: presenter + guest (2 voices) | ~470 words (≈3 min) | **10 · Richtig/Falsch**, in text order | **twice** | 1 min reading time before | [S1][s1] p. 15, 42–43; [S3][s3] |
| 3 Selektives Verstehen | Catch one specific detail per text | 5 short monologues: answering-machine message, cinema programme info, weather report, on-train announcement, in-store announcement | ~45–95 words each (≈20–40 s) | **5 · Richtig/Falsch** (1 per text) | **twice each** | – | [S1][s1] p. 16, 43–44; [S3][s3] |

- Answer marks on the sheet are **+ (richtig) / – (falsch)**. [S1][s1] p. 14–16
- The audio is **not stopped** during the subtest. [S1][s1] p. 29
- The official mock audio file runs **25:00 min** in all, including instructions, reading pauses and repeats (measured). [S3][s3]

### 3d. Schriftlicher Ausdruck (30 min)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reply to an email you received | **Informal or semi-formal email.** The mock has a personal email from a friend (~80 words) as the stimulus. | No word count given | **1 free text, 4 Leitpunkte** (cover all four) | – | 30 min | [S1][s1] p. 5, 17, 36–38 |

- The candidate decides the order of the points and adds a fitting Betreff, Anrede, Einleitung and Schluss (task-sheet guidance). [S1][s1] p. 17
- Letter features (sender, address, date) are **not** required for an email. [S1][s1] p. 37
- **No choice of two tasks** is printed in the mock (the DTZ offers A/B). [S1][s1] p. 17

### 3e. Mündlicher Ausdruck (~15 min, pair)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |

## 4. Scoring

**Points and weighting** [S1][s1] p. 39

| Subtest | Teil | Items | Max points | Per item (derived) | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leseverstehen | 1 / 2 / 3 | 5 / 5 / 10 | 25 / 25 / 25 = **75** | 5 / 5 / 2.5 | 25 % |
| Sprachbausteine | 1 / 2 | 10 / 10 | 15 / 15 = **30** | 1.5 | 10 % |
| Hörverstehen | 1 / 2 / 3 | 5 / 10 / 5 | 25 / 25 / 25 = **75** | 5 / 2.5 / 5 | 25 % |
| Schriftlicher Ausdruck | email | 1 | **45** | – | 15 % |
| **Teilergebnis I (written)** | | | **225** | | 75 % |
| Mündlicher Ausdruck | 1 / 2 / 3 | – | 15 / 30 / 30 = **75** | – | 25 % |
| **Gesamt** | | | **300** | | 100 % |

**Writing** [S1][s1] p. 36–38: 3 criteria, each A/B/C/D = 5/3/1/0 points: **I Aufgabenbewältigung** (how many of the 4 Leitpunkte are handled adequately: 4→A, 3→B, 2→C, ≤1→D), **II Kommunikative Gestaltung** (cohesion, register, connectors, range; A is withheld if, e.g., most sentences start with *Ich/Wir* or the points stand unconnected), **III Formale Richtigkeit**. The sum (max 15) is **×3 = 45**. "Thema verfehlt" means D on all criteria. A wrong situation (Situierung) means D on criterion I only. Two licensed raters score each text; the second rating prevails.

**Speaking** [S1][s1] p. 32–35: each Teil is scored separately on 4 criteria: **Ausdrucksfähigkeit**, **Aufgabenbewältigung**, **Formale Richtigkeit** (A/B/C/D = 4/3/1/0 in Teil 1; 8/6/2/0 in Teil 2 and 3), and **Aussprache/Intonation** (3/2/1/0; 6/4/2/0). That gives Teil 1 max 15 and Teil 2 and 3 max 30 each. Two examiners score independently, then agree. Asking back and helping each other count positively.

**Pass rule** [S1][s1] p. 40: **60 % in the written exam (135/225) AND 60 % in the oral exam (45/75).** Only then are the parts added up and graded:

| Points | Note |
|---|---|
| 270–300 | sehr gut |
| 240–269.5 | gut |
| 210–239.5 | befriedigend |
| 180–209.5 | ausreichend |
| 0–179.5 | nicht bestanden |

A pass gets a certificate. A fail gets a Ergebnisbogen. A failed or missed part (written or oral) can be retaken alone in the same or the following calendar year. Only the retake result counts. [S1][s1] p. 40

## 5. Speaking: the pair format in detail (for our TTS partner)

Sources: [S1][s1] p. 30–35.

- **Setting:** two candidates (A and B) and two licensed examiners. One examiner is the **Interlokutor** (moderator); the other is the **Assessor** (observer). Candidates sit facing each other and talk to each other, not to the examiners.
- **Single candidate:** if only one person registers, one examiner takes the second candidate's role. So a "partner" played by someone else is an official setup. This is the closest real-world model for our TTS partner.
- **Interlokutor's role:** opens with greeting and introductions, says the exam has 3 parts, starts Teil 1 (usually with A), moves to Teil 2 (asks **B** first what their sheet says, then A), invites the discussion ("How do you both see it?"), moves to Teil 3 (asks **A** for proposals first, then B for opinion and proposals), and ends the exam. **Speaks as little as possible.** Steps in only if one candidate dominates, one is passive (then addresses the quieter one directly), or the talk stalls. Uses open W-questions or repeats what was said. In Teil 1 the examiner may add an extra topic (e.g. weekend, hobbies).
- **What the partner does per Teil:**
  - **Teil 1:** introduces themself along the cue topics, **asks the candidate questions** and answers theirs. It is a real exchange, not a monologue.
  - **Teil 2:** the partner holds the **opposing opinion sheet**, summarises it, reacts to the candidate's summary, gives their own opinion and experience, and asks back.
  - **Teil 3:** the partner makes proposals with reasons, reacts to the candidate's proposals (agree, object, counter-propose), divides the tasks ("who does what") and works toward **one agreed plan**.
- **Scoring is per candidate.** The partner's quality doesn't count, but "Gesprächsbeteiligung" and strategies (asking back, helping) do. So our partner must leave real turns open and react to what the candidate said.

**Implications for the simulator** (design notes, not facts):
- Two TTS voices: an **Interlokutor** voice (transitions, rescue prompts) and a **Partner** voice of a different gender/timbre (`data/tts-voices.json`).
- The partner's turns must be **dynamic** (reacting to the candidate's transcript) or at least branch between agree, disagree and ask back. A fixed script turns this into solo prompts, the exact gap we criticise in TestGerman.
- Give the 20-min preparation phase with all three sheets before the talk starts.

## 6. Kurztest composition proposal

This mirrors the DTZ cut in `lib/exam/blueprint.ts`: real-length texts with fewer items per block, every Teil present, ~10 items per closed skill.

**Where Sprachbausteine go:** inside the **Lesen Kurztest**. In the real exam, LV and SB share one 90-minute block and the same answer sheet, so candidates experience them as one sitting. Keep SB as its own `skill` value in the data model, so scoring can apply its lower weight (1.5 pts/item vs 2.5–5 for LV).

**Lesen + Sprachbausteine: 10 items.** Every Teil gets 2 items. That sits between item share (LV 50 %) and point share (LV 71 %).

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Keep from real format |
|---|---|---|---|
| LV 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 short texts (~65 words) + **4 headlines** (keeps the 2:1 headline-to-text ratio) |
| LV 2 | 1 | 2 | full ~320-word article, 2 a/b/c items |
| LV 3 | 1 | 2 | 2 situations + **3 ads**, "x" allowed |
| SB 1 | 1 | 2 | one ~150-word letter, 2 a/b/c gaps (the other gaps pre-filled) |
| SB 2 | 1 | 2 | one ~170-word letter, 2 gaps, **3-word pool** (keeps 15:10 distractor ratio) |

**Hören: 10 items.** All three Teile are worth 25 points each, while item counts run 5/10/5. A 3/4/3 split balances both.

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Plays | Keep from real format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 1 each | **once** | short vox-pop intro + 3 speakers, 30 s reading time |
| 2 | 1 | 4 | **twice** | full-length (~3 min) radio interview, 2 voices, 1 min reading time |
| 3 | 3 | 1 each | **twice each** | 3 different short text types (phone message, announcement, weather/radio) |

**Schreiben:** 1 full task. Reply to an email, 4 Leitpunkte, 30 min. AI rubric = official criteria I–III (5/3/1/0 each, ×3).

**Sprechen:** all three Teile with the TTS Interlokutor and partner (§5). Teil 1 ~3 min, Teil 2 ~6 min, Teil 3 ~6 min, with preparation before. AI rubric = the 4 official criteria per Teil.
