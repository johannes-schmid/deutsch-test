**DTZ (Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer, A2·B1).** Goethe-Institut and telc developed it jointly for the BMI [G1]. telc's own 2021 brochure still lists it as a telc exam [P1]. telc administered it until 31.12.2022. Since 01.01.2023 the DTZ has been administered by **g.a.s.t.** [L2][G2]. It is **not** on telc.net's current German exam list [T1]. uni-assist now lists it as "Zertifikat DTZ B1/A2 (g.a.s.t.)" [H3].

**Remote tests.** telc offers no remote test for German. Remote certificates are not accepted for visa or residence purposes [T21].

## 3. Skeleton formats of the other exams

### 3a. telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer (no. 1069). Source: Übungstest 1 (2021) [P1], product page [T2]

Written part, ~70 min, in this order:

| Subtest | Teil | Task type | Items | Points | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sprachbausteine | – | MC, vocabulary/grammar | 10 | 10 × 1.5 = 15 | 10 min |
| 2 Hören | A | Selective listening, R/F | 4 | 4 × 3 = 12 | ~15 min total for Hören + Hören und antworten (set by the recording) |
| | B | Detail, MC (numbers/letters) | 5 | 5 × 1 = 5 | |
| 3 Hören und antworten | A | Situational responses, matching | 3 | 3 × 2 = 6 | |
| | B | Situational responses, matching | 4 | 4 × 2 = 8 | |
| 4 Lesen | A | Global, matching | 3 | 3 × 3 = 9 | 30 min |
| | B | Detail, R/F | 3 | 3 × 3 = 9 | |
| | C | Selective, MC | 3 | 3 × 2 = 6 | |
| 5 Schreiben | A | Fill in a form | – | 2.5 | 15 min |
| | B | Short message | – | 7.5 | |

Oral part, ~15 min, **group of 4** (2 or 3, or exceptionally 1, allowed). No preparation time. Two examiners: one interlocutor and one assessor.

| Teil | Task | Material | Time | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Introduce yourself, spell, say numbers | Keyword sheet | ~3 min | 6 |
| B | Ask for and give information (2 everyday topics) | 2 cards per candidate | ~4 min | 7 |
| C | Requests, suggestions, offers, advice, and responding to them | 2 cards per candidate | ~4 min | 7 |

- **Total 100 points. Pass at 60 points (60 %)** [P1].
- No Teilprüfung; paper only [T2].
- A2 for this exam has no analogue: it is pass/fail A1, not scaled.

### 3b. telc Deutsch A2·B1 (no. 1060). Source: Übungstest 1 (5060-B00-010102, 2024) [P2], Produktblatt [P3]

Written part, 125 min in total [P2]:

| Subtest | Teil | Task | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 1 | Small ads ↔ situations, matching | 5 | 45 min |
| | 2 | Short text, detail: 1 R/F + 3 MC | 4 | |
| | 3 | Emails, global, matching | 4 | |
| | 4 | Longer text, detail, MC | 5 | |
| Sprachbausteine – Lesen und Schreiben | 1 | Text structure, MC | 10 | 35 min |
| | 2 | Understand an email: 1 R/F + 1 MC | 2 | |
| | 3 | Reply to the email (writing) | 1 | |
| Hören – Hören und Schreiben | 1 | Short everyday conversation, matching | 5 | ~35 min, recording not interrupted |
| | 2 | Work conversation, R/F (played **once**, Übungstest p. 13) | 5 | |
| | 3 | Announcements, R/F | 5 | |
| | 4 | Opinions on a topic, matching (opinions played **once**) | 5 | |
| | 5 | Answering-machine messages → write a note | 5 | |
| Schreiben | – | Short message replying to a question | 1 | 10 min |

Oral part: **pair exam**, ~15 min, no preparation time. A single exam is allowed only with an odd number of candidates. Plan 20 min per pair [P2].

| Teil | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Introduce yourself (keyword sheet) | ~1.5 min per candidate |
| 1B | Answer the examiner's follow-up questions | ~1 min per candidate |
| 2 | Plan something together, as colleagues | ~5 min |
| 3 | Give and justify opinions on a question | ~5 min |

**Scoring.** 240 points, 60 per skill (25 % each) [P2]:

- Lesen and Hören: 20 items × 3 points each.
- Schreiben (60) is the sum of:
  - Sprachbausteine: 10 × 0.5
  - Hören-und-Schreiben notes: 5 × 1
  - Email: 15
  - Short message: 5
  - Cross-task criteria II–IV: 30
- Sprechen (60): Teil 1 = 6, Teil 2 = 12, Teil 3 = 12, criteria II–IV = 30.
- Criterion bands are labelled "B1 gut erfüllt / B1 erfüllt / A2 gut erfüllt / A2 erfüllt / unter A2".

**Result rule** [P2]:

- **B1** needs:
  - ≥ 168/240 in total (70 %), and
  - ≥ 42/60 (70 %) in 3 of the 4 skills, and
  - ≥ 24/60 (40 %) in every skill.
- **A2** needs:
  - ≥ 96/240 in total (40 %), and
  - ≥ 24/60 (40 %) in 3 of the 4 skills, and
  - ≥ 6/60 (10 %) in every skill.
- The certificate shows the total and the part results [P3].

> Product note: this is the closest telc analogue to the DTZ. It is scaled B1/A2 with a pair Sprechen. But it differs in parts, item counts and timings. Sprachbausteine and Hören-und-Schreiben score as **Schreiben**, and the thresholds are 70 %/40 %. It needs its own blueprint.

### 3c. telc Deutsch A2+ Beruf. Source: Übungstest 1 (2nd ed. 2018) [P4], Handbuch (3rd ed. 2017) [P7], product page [T4]

| Subtest | Content | Items | Points | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Lesen | Job ads, notices, minutes, info sheets; 3-option MC | 15 | 15 × 1 | 60 min total for 1 + 2 (spec: Lesen 40 + Strukturen 20) |
| 2 Strukturen/Wortschatz | Business correspondence; 3-option MC | 10 (2 × 5) | 10 × 1 | |
| 3 Hören | Task 1: work conversation in 3 sections, R/F (played twice). Task 2: 5 statements, MC | 15 R/F + 5 MC | 20 × 1.5 = 30 | 30 min |
| 4 Schreiben | (1) semi-formal reply; (2) note to a colleague; 3 Leitpunkte each | 2 | 2 × 7.5 = 15 | 30 min |
| 5 Sprechen | **Pair**, no preparation, no dictionary. (1) talk about yourself, ~3–4 min, 6 pts; (2) talk about a job, picture input, 5–6 min, 12 pts; (3) plan something together, 5–6 min, 12 pts | – | 30 | 15 min |

- **100 points.** Pass needs ≥ 1 point in every subtest and **≥ 60 points in total**.
- Grades: 90.25–100 sehr gut · 80.25–90 gut · 70.25–80 befriedigend · 60–70 ausreichend [P4].
- Digital, hybrid and paper; no Teilprüfung [T4].
- Note: the product page shows "Lesen 4 Teile, Sprachbausteine 2 Teile, Hören 2, Schreiben 2" [T4]. That fits the 2018 Übungstest's 15 + 10 + 20 items. The exact split of the 15 reading items across 4 Teile is UNVERIFIED.

### 3d. telc Deutsch B1+ Beruf. Source: Übungstest 1 (2017 print) [P5], product page [T5]

| Subtest | Teil | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leseverstehen | 1 global, matching · 2 detail, MC · 3 selective, matching | 5 · 5 · 10 | 90 min total for Lesen + Sprachbausteine |
| Sprachbausteine | 1 MC · 2 matching | 10 · 10 | |
| Hörverstehen | 1 global, R/F · 2 detail, R/F · 3 selective, R/F | 5 · 10 · 5 | ~30 min |
| Schriftlicher Ausdruck | Informal or semi-formal letter, 4 Leitpunkte | 1 | 30 min |
| Mündlicher Ausdruck | **20 min preparation**, then ~15 min **pair** (or individual). 1 Kontaktaufnahme · 2 Gespräch über ein Thema · 3 Gemeinsam eine Aufgabe lösen | – | ~15 min |

- **300 points**: written 225 (75 %), oral 75 (25 %). Writing = 45 points (3 criteria × 15). Oral = Teil 1: 15 + Teil 2: 30 + Teil 3: 30.
- Pass: **≥ 60 % written (135) AND ≥ 60 % oral (45)**, total ≥ 180.
- Grades: 270–300 sehr gut · 240–269.5 gut · 210–239.5 befriedigend · 180–209.5 ausreichend.
- A failed written-only or oral-only part may be retaken until the end of the following calendar year [P5].
- The skeleton is the same as telc Deutsch B1 / Zertifikat Deutsch, but with work content [R1][P5].

### 3e. telc Deutsch B1·B2 Beruf. Source: Übungstest 1 (2nd ed. 2017) [P6], product page [T6]

| Subtest | Teil | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hören | 1 phone messages, MC · 2 everyday/work conversations, 5 R/F + 5 MC · 3 meetings/interviews, MC · 4 opinions, matching | 4 · 10 · 6 · 3 | 25 min |
| Lesen | 1 emails, global, matching · 2 forum posts, matching · 3 instructions/circulars, MC · 4 regulatory texts, R/F | 4 · 5 · 6 · 3 | 60 min (Lesen + Sprachbausteine) |
| Sprachbausteine | 1 external correspondence, matching · 2 internal correspondence, MC | 8 · 10 | |
| Schreiben | 1 work communication, 3 content points · 2 note/email between colleagues, 3 content points | 2 | 45 min |
| Sprechen | **20 min preparation**, then ~16 min, usually **pair**. 1A experiences/opinions (pictures) · 1B follow-up questions · 2A short presentation · 2B ask and answer follow-up questions · 3 solve a task together | – | ~16 min |

**Scoring** [P6]:

- Schreiben: 4 criteria × 5 per text = 20 per text, 40 in total. **B2 = 30–40, B1 = 14–29.**
- Sprechen: 100 points. Task completion: 1A 5, 1B 5, 2A 15, 2B 5, Teil 3 20. Criteria II–V: 50. **B2 = 75–100, B1 = 35–74.5.**
- Certificate:
  - Sprechen must reach the certificate level.
  - Then **one** of the two other parts (Hören/Lesen/Sprachbausteine, or Schreiben) must also reach that level.
  - Minimum floors apply: ≥ 19 points in H/L/SB, ≥ 8 in Schreiben.
  - "Unter B1" in Sprechen = no certificate.
- Hybrid and paper; no Teilprüfung [T6].

### 3g. telc Deutsch B1·B2 Pflege
- **Written:**
  - Hören 25 min, 4 parts, 18 items: phone messages, everyday care talk (Visite, Übergabe, patient and
    relative conversations), team meeting, opinions. Same item formats as Beruf.
  - Lesen + Sprachbausteine 60 min: e-mails, Pflegeplanung, mixed texts (Beipackzettel, circulars, legal
    texts), semi-formal info texts. Then Sprachbausteine: connections in reports (8 matching) and
    specialist vocabulary in reports (10 MC).
  - Schreiben 30 min: **1 task, chosen from 2**: Aufnahmebericht or Biografiebericht.
- **Oral:** ~16 min, pair, **20 min prep**.
  - 1A: experiences/opinions. 1B: examiner questions.
  - 2A: short talk, chosen from 2. 2B: examiner questions.
  - 3: discussion of a controversial topic using sample statements.
- **Bands:** Hören/Lesen/Sprachbausteine 59 pts (B2 43–59, B1 27–42). Schreiben 20 pts
  (B2 15–20, B1 7–14, unter B1 ≤6). Certificate logic as for Beruf.

## 4. Which certificate for which purpose

> Legal facts below are quoted from the source. Where a source doesn't settle a point, it is marked UNVERIFIED. Don't publish any of this without a `Stand:` date.

### Ehegattennachzug (A1, "einfache Deutschkenntnisse")
- Legal level: § 2 Abs. 9 AufenthG defines "einfache deutsche Sprachkenntnisse" as A1 [L4]. The requirement and its exceptions are in § 28 Abs. 1 S. 5 and § 30 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 2 AufenthG (cited in [B1]).
- BAMF flyer (**Stand 02/2021**), quoted: the certificate must rest on a standardised ALTE exam. "Dies trifft derzeit für folgende Sprachzertifikate zu: ‚Start Deutsch 1' des Goethe-Instituts oder der telc GmbH, ‚Grundstufe Deutsch 1' des ÖSD, ‚TestDaF'" [B1].
- The embassy where the visa is filed decides alone [B1]. Embassy sheets add that the certificate should generally be no older than 1 year [B2]. That validity rule is **UNVERIFIED** as a general rule; it comes from one embassy sheet.
- **telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer is NOT named** in the BAMF/AA list. Only telc *Start Deutsch 1* is named [B1]. Whether embassies accept the A1 Zuwanderer certificate is **UNVERIFIED**. A1 für Zuwanderer is aimed at migrants already in Germany [P1].

### Niederlassungserlaubnis / Einbürgerung (B1, "ausreichende Kenntnisse")
- § 2 Abs. 11 AufenthG: "ausreichende" = B1 [L4].
- § 9 Abs. 2 S. 1 Nr. 7 AufenthG requires them for the Niederlassungserlaubnis. Satz 2: they count as proven when an Integrationskurs was successfully completed [L5].
- § 10 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 6 + Abs. 4 S. 1 StAG: the candidate must meet "die Anforderungen einer Sprachprüfung der Stufe B 1 des GER". There is a hardship reduction (Abs. 4a) and an illness/disability/age exception (Abs. 6) [L6].
- **BAMF list:** I found **no BAMF-published list of accepted B1 certificates** for Einbürgerung or the Niederlassungserlaubnis. **UNVERIFIED** whether one exists. The current official list found is the **Bavarian "Positivliste von Sprachnachweisen"**, an annex to the Bavarian application notes on the StAG, **Stand 27.06.2024 (BayStMI)** [L2]. Munich uses the same version [L2b]. No 2025/2026 version was found. telc entries at B1:
  - Zertifikat Deutsch (telc Deutsch B1)
  - telc Deutsch B1 Schule
  - telc Deutsch B1+ Beruf
  - **telc Deutsch A2-B1 (only if B1 is certified)**
  - DTZ A2/B1 (only if B1 is certified; telc until 31.12.2022, g.a.s.t. since 01.01.2023)
  - DTB B1 (BAMF/telc)
  - As "höherwertig": telc B2, B1-B2 Pflege, B1-B2 Beruf, B2 Medizin Zugangsprüfung, B2+ Beruf, B2-C1 Medizin, B2-C1 Beruf, B2-C1 Medizin Fachsprachprüfung, C1, C1 Hochschule, C1 Beruf, DTB B2/C1, C2 [L2].
- Other Länder may use different lists. **UNVERIFIED.**

### Pflege (Anerkennung as Pflegefachfrau/-mann): B2
- **Bavaria** (LfP, Stand 10.06.2025):
  - B2 certificate required; no separate Fachsprachprüfung at present.
  - Accepts "telc Deutsch B2" and "telc Deutsch B2 Pflege auf der Grundlage der Prüfung telc Deutsch B1∙B2 Pflege", plus DTB B2.
  - telc certificates from Serbian/Bosnian telc institutes are not accepted from 23.06.2022 [L1].
- **Hesse** (HLfGP, Stand 24.07.2024) accepts **only** these telc certificates: telc Deutsch B2 (or higher), B2 Beruf / B2 Pflege (or higher), B2+ Beruf, DTB B2. It also accepts Goethe, TestDaF, ÖSD and ECL [L7].
- The B1 level of B1·B2 Pflege is **not** enough for Anerkennung in these two Länder [L1][L7]. Other Länder: **UNVERIFIED**.

### Ärzte (Approbation): B2 general + C1 Fachsprache
- **Baden-Württemberg** (RP, page undated):
  - B2 general certificate from an ALTE provider (telc is named).
  - **C1 Fachsprache**, tested by the Landesärztekammer (~€250–450).
  - Private alternatives accepted only if another Land accepts them as its Fachsprachprüfung. Currently: FaMed (after 13.05.2024), **telc Deutsch B2/C1 Medizin Fachsprachprüfung**, and the ÖAK test [L3].
- Brandenburg (04/2024) requires a B2 certificate before the Kammer's Fachsprachtest [L8].
- Rules differ by Land, and which Länder run their own exams versus accept telc is **UNVERIFIED** beyond BW and Brandenburg.

### University entry: C1 Hochschule
- **RO-DT** (HRK/KMK framework, version of 2019 [H1]). § 8 Abs. 2 (d): holders of a passed "telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule" are exempt from the DSH/TestDaF proof.
- **uni-assist** (Stand 02.2026) puts telc C1 Hochschule in **category A**, together with DSH-2, TestDaF TDN 4 and Goethe C2. It cites § 8 Abs. 1 i.V.m. Abs. 2d RO-DT [H3]. Other telc C1 certificates fall in a lower category (B).
- A **newer RO-DT exists** (HRK 04.11.2025, KMK 07.11.2025), cited by HAW Landshut, which still lists telc C1 Hochschule as valid for Bachelor and Master (Stand 05.02.2026) [H2]. The paragraph number in the 2025 version is UNVERIFIED.
- Universities still set local rules (RO-DT § 8 Abs. 3) [H1]. For example, Landshut rejects certificates older than 2 years [H2].
- **"Which universities accept it":** in principle every HRK member following the RO-DT. Per-university acceptance or exclusions are **UNVERIFIED**; check each Zulassungsordnung.

## 6. Level comparison: telc German A1 → C1

| | Start Deutsch 1 / A1 (1070) | Start Deutsch 2 / A2 (1090) | Zertifikat Deutsch / B1 (1061) | telc Deutsch B2 (1023) | telc Deutsch C1 / C1 Hochschule (1029 / 1032) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Written time | Hören ~20 + Lesen/Schreiben 45 = ~65 min | Hören ~20 + Lesen/Schreiben 50 = 70 min | Lesen + SB 90 + Hören ~30 + Schreiben 30 | LV + SB 90 + HV ~20 + SA 30 = 140 min | LV + SB 90 + break 20 + HV ~40 + SA 70 ≈ 220 min |
| Sprachbausteine | – | – | 2 parts | 2 parts (10 MC + 10 from a 15-word pool) | 1 part, 22 items, 4-option MC |
| Hören plays | Teil 1 and 3 twice, Teil 2 once | Teil 1 and 3 twice, Teil 2 once | Teil 1 once, Teil 2 and 3 twice | **all once** | **all once** |
| Writing | form (5 fields) + ~30-word message | form (5 fields) + ~40-word letter | 1 e-mail, 4 Leitpunkte, 30 min | 1 semi-formal e-mail, ≥150 words, choose A/B | 1 argumentative text ~350 (C1) / ≥350 (HS) words, choose 1 of 2, 70 min |
| Speaking | ~15 min, usually 4 candidates, **no prep** | ~15 min, pair, **no prep** | ~15 min, pair, 20 min prep | ~15 min, pair, 20 min prep; experience talk / text discussion / planning | ~16 min, pair, 20 min prep; presentation / summary + questions / discussion |
| Result | points + grade | grade on 60 pts | grade | grade on 300; ≥60% written and oral | grade on 214; ≥128 total and ≥60% each part |
| Partial retake | no | no | yes (12 months) | yes (12 months) | yes (12 months) |
| Delivery (S-LIST) | Paper, Digital (product page says paper only, a conflict) | Paper, Digital (product page: paper only) | Digital, Hybrid, Paper | Digital, Hybrid, Paper | C1: Hybrid, Paper (page conflict); HS: Hybrid, Paper, Digital |

- **The steep step is B2 → C1:**
  - The Hören text type shifts to a lecture with note-taking (open answers).
  - Reading adds „nicht im Text“ and gap-sentence reconstruction.
  - Writing more than doubles (≥150 → ~350 words; 30 → 70 min).
  - Speaking moves from guided pair tasks to monologic presentation + summarising + debate.
  - Weighting becomes almost even (22.5% per skill, 10% Sprachbausteine).

### Have A1, A2, B1 changed since 2024?

- **Start Deutsch 1 / A1:** no change found.
  - The current free Übungstest is 10th ed. 2017 (zip) and 11th ed. 2021 (shop).
  - The product page still lists Hören 3 parts ~20 min, Lesen 3 + Schreiben 2 parts 45 min, Sprechen 3 parts.
  - No „neues Format“ notice [S-A1].
- **Start Deutsch 2 / A2:** a **new edition was published in Dec 2024** (10th ed., „Barrierefrei“ =
  accessible layout). **Format unchanged** compared with the 8th ed. 2017:
  - Same parts and play counts: Hören 1 notes, played twice; Hören 2 MC, once; Hören 3 matching, twice.
  - Lesen 3 parts: MC, R/F, matching ads.
  - Schreiben 2 parts, the second ~40 words.
  - Sprechen: sich vorstellen / Alltagsgespräch / etwas aushandeln, scored 3 + 6 + 6.
  - 60 points in total.
  - The only visible difference: the 2017 table listed 10 min „Formalitäten“ (total 80 min); 2024 lists
    70 min [S-A2].
- **Zertifikat Deutsch / B1:** no change found.
  - The free Übungstest is the 13th ed. 2020 (zip repackaged 2023-03).
  - The product page lists Lesen 3 + SB 2 parts in 90 min, Hören 3 parts ~30 min, Schreiben 30 min, and
    pair speaking with 20 min prep [S-B1].
  - **Related:** telc launched a **new two-level exam telc Deutsch A2·B1 (no. 1060)** in 2023, with its own
    format (Lesen 4 parts in 45 min, Sprachbausteine, Lesen und Schreiben, Hören, Hören und Schreiben,
    Schreiben). It is a separate product, not a revision of Zertifikat Deutsch [S-A2B1].
- **Cross-cutting 2024–2026 changes:**
  1. **Prüfungsregularien valid from 15.04.2025:** the partial-retake window is now 12 months from the
     result sheet, replacing the older calendar-year wording. Partial crediting applies only to B1, B2, C1
     and C2 [S-REG25].
  2. **Digital and hybrid delivery are spreading** (DIGItelc 2.0; digital exam numbers = 8xxx). Hybrid and
     digital have identical content. Speaking in both runs by video conference with examiners, not as a
     recorded computer test [S-DIGI].
