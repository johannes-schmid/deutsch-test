# ÖSD Zertifikat A1 (ZA1): situations in the official set (do-not-reuse list)

**Stand: 24.10.2026** · paraphrased from the only public set:

- **MS** = *ZA1 Modellsatz* (2026 edition; identical to the 2024 Modellsatzbroschüre, audio recorded ~2017) [S5/S1 in `oesd-a1.md`]. The transcript in the Broschüre (§3.3) and Scribe on the MP3s agree.
- Cites are printed pages ("S.") of the Modellsatz PDF.

The official texts are copyright. This file lists only the **situation and the detail each task tests**, in our words, so the
generator can avoid them. Never put official wording into a prompt or a draft. TestGerman and our free tests are listed at the
end, because set 1 must avoid their storylines too.
