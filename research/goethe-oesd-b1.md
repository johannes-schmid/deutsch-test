## 3. Sections

Notation: **R/F** = Richtig/Falsch · **abc** = 3-option multiple choice · lengths in words are my counts from the Goethe Übungssatz [S4] (approximate, ±10%). Times per Teil are the recommended *Arbeitszeit* printed on the candidate sheets [S4, S5]; only the module total is binding.

### 3.1 Lesen: 65 min, 30 items, 5 Teile, any order

| Teil | Task (Prüfungsziel) | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Korrespondenz lesen | One longer personal text: a personal email or blog post about one's own life, incl. feelings | ~330 words | 6 · R/F (+ example 0) | – | 10 min | S4, S5 |
| 2 | Information und Argumentation verstehen | **Two** shorter press texts (newspaper/magazine report), public/work/school domain | ~190–220 words each (corrected 2026-10-24 from ~250, §8.2) | 6 = 2 texts × 3 · abc (+ example 0 on text 1) | – | 20 min | S4, S5 |
| 3 | Zur Orientierung lesen | 10 short ads (a–j) from various media on one theme, plus 7 people's situations (+ example) | ads ~20–50 words each (~330 in all) + ~125 words of framing and situations (corrected 2026-10-24, §8.2) | 7 · matching, ad letter or **"0"** (exactly one situation has no matching ad; each ad is used at most once; the example's ad is used up) | – | 10 min | S4, S5 |
| 4 | Information und Argumentation verstehen | 7 readers' letters / forum comments (+ example) on one controversial question, each signed with name, age and city (D/A/CH) | ~50–70 words each, ~470 total | 7 · **Ja/Nein** (is the writer *for* the proposal?) | – | 15 min | S4, S5 |
| 5 | Schriftliche Anweisungen verstehen | One rules or instruction text (e.g. Hausordnung, Anleitung, Nutzungsbedingungen) | ~250 words | 4 · abc (sentence stem + 3 endings) | – | 10 min | S4, S5 |

**Instruction pattern (paraphrased):**

- Module intro: five parts, start with any of them, only one correct answer per item, transfer answers to the answer sheet within the time, no dictionaries or phones.
- Each Teil: *Lesen Sie [Text/Situationen] und die Aufgaben X bis Y* plus the decision prompt, with one worked example (0) at the start of Teile 1–4.
- Teil 4 names the question to judge ("Ist die Person für …? Ja oder Nein").
- Teil 3 states the "use each ad once, one situation gets 0" rule. Teil 5 opens with a one-sentence situation framing (you read the rules of a place you just joined). [S4, S5]
- Answers are first marked on the sheets, then transferred; plan ~5 min for this inside the 65 min. In the digital version answers go straight onto the platform. [S1, S3]

### 3.2 Hören: ca. 40 min, 30 items, 4 Teile, fixed order (audio-driven)

| Teil | Task (Prüfungsziel) | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ankündigungen, Durchsagen und Anweisungen verstehen | 5 short texts (+ example). Mix of voicemail / mobile-box messages (private and from businesses or property managers), radio announcement or call-in promo, station or tourist **Durchsage**, advert. Mostly 1 speaker. | ~65–85 words, 26–42 s each (corrected 2026-10-24 from ~70–100; measured, §8.3) | 10 = 5 × 2 · per text **1 R/F** (gist/situation) **+ 1 abc** (detail) | **2×** | 10 s reading per text | S4, S5 |
| 2 | Als Zuschauer/Zuhörer im Publikum verstehen | 1 lecture-like monologue to an audience (e.g. a guide or organiser briefing participants), framed by a one-line situation | ~400–415 words, 2:36–3:16 (measured) | 5 · abc | **1×** | 60 s reading before | S4, S5 |
| 3 | Gespräche zwischen Muttersprachlern / kompetenten Sprechenden verstehen | 1 informal everyday dialogue, 2 people (named, m+f), overheard in a public place (e.g. on a tram) | ~455–460 words, 2:50–3:19 (corrected from ~480; measured) | 7 · R/F | **1×** | 60 s reading before | S4, S5 |
| 4 | Radiosendungen und Tonaufnahmen verstehen | 1 radio **discussion between 3 people**: moderator + 2 guests (e.g. expert + affected person). Names and roles shown above the item grid. | ~750–800 words, 4:48–5:15 per play (measured) | 8 · **matching each statement to one of the 3 speakers (a/b/c)** (+ example 0) | **2×** | 60 s reading before | S4, S5 |

**Instruction pattern (paraphrased):**

- Module intro: four parts; read the items first, then listen; one correct answer each; **5 minutes at the end to transfer answers**; no aids. [S4]
- Each Teil's audio states how many texts, **how many times you hear them** and how many items, then gives the reading time ("10 s" for the example and each text in Teil 1, "60 s" before Teile 2–4). The audio holds all instructions. [S1, S4, S5]
- Seconds per clip: ~~TODO(hannes): unverified~~ **measured 2026-10-24** on the official Modellsatz and Übungssatz audio: Teil 1 26–42 s per text, Teil 2 2:36–3:16, Teil 3 2:50–3:19, Teil 4 4:48–5:15 per play; pace 105–169 words/min. Full timing map in §8.3.
- Speakers: German, Austrian and Swiss, standard language with a light regional accent, no dialect (stated by ÖSD [S5]).

### 3.3 Schreiben: 60 min, 3 Aufgaben, any order

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interaktion: personal message to keep in touch | **Informal email** to a friend about something you did or experienced | **ca. 80 words** | 1 free text · 3 bullet Leitpunkte with fixed functions: *Beschreiben Sie … / Begründen Sie … / Machen Sie einen Vorschlag …* | – | 20 min | S4, S5 |
| 2 | Produktion: give a personal opinion | **Diskussionsbeitrag** in an online guest book after a TV discussion; one stimulus post (~45 words) is shown | **ca. 80 words** | 1 free text · opinion on the topic (describe, give reasons, compare, etc.) | – | 25 min | S4, S5 |
| 3 | Interaktion: message to regulate an action | **Short formal/polite email** to a named person you know only formally (course leader, teacher), e.g. apologise and explain why you can't come | **ca. 40 words** | 1 free text · apologise / ask for something; salutation and closing required | – | 15 min | S4, S5 |

**Instruction pattern (paraphrased):**

- Situation in 1–2 sentences, then the bullet points or task, then *Schreiben Sie eine E-Mail / Ihre Meinung (circa N Wörter)*.
- Aufgabe 1 adds "cover all three points; mind the structure: Anrede, Einleitung, order of points, Schluss".
- Aufgabe 3 adds "don't forget Anrede and Gruß". [S4, S5]

### 3.4 Sprechen: 15 min prep (alone, notes allowed) + ca. 15 min pair exam

| Teil | Task | Situation | Length | Format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (Intro) | Warm-up with the moderator: name, origin, how long learning German, etc. | – | – | **not scored** | – | ca. 1 min | S1, S4 |
| 1 | **Gemeinsam etwas planen** (Interaktion) | A shared scenario card (e.g. you won a trip together, visiting a sick classmate) with **4 Leitpunkte** as questions + an open "…" | – | Dialogue with partner: make proposals, react to the partner's proposals, decide together | – | ca. 3 min (2–3) | S1, S3, S4 |
| 2 | **Ein Thema präsentieren** (Produktion) | Each candidate picks **1 of 2 topics** and presents it with **5 given slides** | – | Monologue | – | ca. 3 min (3–4) per candidate | S1, S4 |
| 3 | **Über ein Thema sprechen** (Interaktion) | After each presentation | – | Listener-partner gives **Rückmeldung + 1 question**; **examiner 2 asks 1 question**; presenter answers | – | ca. 2 min (1–2) per candidate | S1, S3, S4 |

The five Teil 2 slides, in fixed order [S4, S5]:

1. Introduce the topic and the structure of the talk.
2. Own situation or experience.
3. Situation in the home country, with examples.
4. Pros and cons plus own opinion, with examples.
5. Close and thank the audience.

## 4. Scoring

| Module | Raw | Reported | Breakdown | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 30 items × 1 | ×3.33 → /100 (conversion table: 18 → 60, 17 → 57) | 1 point per item, no partial credit | **≥ 60** |
| Hören | 30 items × 1 | ×3.33 → /100 | as Lesen | **≥ 60** |
| Schreiben | criteria A–E | /100 | **Aufgabe 1: 40** (Erfüllung, Kohärenz, Wortschatz, Strukturen at 10 each; steps 10/7.5/5/2.5/0). **Aufgabe 2: 40** (same). **Aufgabe 3: 20** (Erfüllung 4, Kohärenz 4, Wortschatz 6, Strukturen 6). Erfüllung = E (0) → the whole Aufgabe scores 0. E is also given below 50% of the word count or when off topic. | **≥ 60** |
| Sprechen | criteria A–E | /100 | **T1: 28** (Erfüllung 8, Interaktion 4, Wortschatz/Register 8, Strukturen 8). **T2: 40** (Erfüllung 12, Kohärenz 4, Wortschatz/Register 12, Strukturen 12). **T2 Erfüllung** is driven by slide coverage: all 5 = A, 3–4 = B, 2 = C, 1 = D. **T3: 16** (Erfüllung only: give feedback, ask, answer). **Aussprache: 16** (whole exam). Warm-up not scored. | **≥ 60** |

Sources: S1 §4–6, S3 §4–6, S4 Bewertungskriterien and Bewertungsbögen.

- **Prädikate per module:** 100–90 sehr gut · 89–80 gut · 79–70 befriedigend · 69–60 ausreichend · 59–0 nicht bestanden. [S1, S3]
- **No total score and no compensation between modules.** Each module passes or fails on its own. [S1, S3]
- **Certificates:** one per passed module; can be combined with later module results; overall certificate rules in §2. Modules can be retaken any number of times. [S1, S2, S3, S6]
- **For our UI:** unlike the DTZ, "bestanden / nicht bestanden" plus the Prädikat **is** the real result format here, per module. For a 10-item Kurztest, show an indicative score on the /100 scale with a clear "Richtwert, kein Prüfungsergebnis" note. Don't call a sample "bestanden".

## 5. Speaking in detail (for the TTS partner simulation)

**Cast in the real exam** [S1, S3, S4]:

- **Examiner 1, moderator:** greets, introduces both examiners, runs the warm-up, explains each Teil briefly, closes the planning, chooses who presents first.
- **Examiner 2:** mostly observes. In Teil 3 asks the presenter one question.
- **Partner:** a fellow candidate who prepared the same Teil 1 card and their own Teil 2 topic. In the single exam, an examiner plays the partner in Teil 1.

**Flow** (from the moderation script in [S4], paraphrased):

1. Welcome, then warm-up questions to each candidate in turn (not scored).
2. **Teil 1.** The moderator gives a one-line task description and invites the pair to start. The candidates talk to each other, not to the examiner, for ~3 min. They take turns making proposals, agreeing or disagreeing with reasons, and countering. They should cover all 4 Leitpunkte (plus their own ideas) and reach decisions. Both should speak about equally long and often [S5]. The moderator asks "thought of everything?" and closes.
3. **Teile 2+3, round A.** The moderator reminds them of an introduction and conclusion and asks them not to read out notes. Candidate 1 presents (~3 min, 5 slides). The moderator asks candidate 2 to listen and prepare a question. Then candidate 2 gives feedback (what they liked, what was new or interesting) and asks one question, and candidate 1 answers. Then examiner 2 asks one question and candidate 1 answers.
4. **Round B:** the same with roles swapped.
5. Close.

**What the TTS partner has to do in our simulation:**

| Teil | Partner (TTS voice B) | Examiners (TTS voices C, D: different voices from the partner) | Candidate (records) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | – | Moderator: greeting + 2–3 warm-up questions | short answers (unscored) |
| 1 | Opens or responds with concrete proposals for each Leitpunkt. Agrees, disagrees with a reason, makes a counter-proposal, and asks the candidate's opinion ("Was meinst du?"). Leaves room for the candidate to lead at least 2 Leitpunkte. Ends with a summary or decision. Pair candidates use **du** with each other. **Resolved 2026-10-24:** the ÖSD Kommentar tells candidates to agree before Teil 1 whether they say *du* or *Sie* [S5 p. 32]. Either is fine; let the candidate pick, default *du*. | Moderator: task intro + closing line | ~5–6 turns, each 15–30 s |
| 2 | Its own ~3-min presentation on its topic (5 slides). Only if we simulate round B. | Moderator: intro + "Bitte beginnen Sie" | ~3-min presentation on the chosen topic |
| 3 | After the candidate's talk: 1–2 sentences of feedback + 1 question **about the candidate's actual talk**. This needs a model step to build the question from the transcript; a fixed question won't do. | Examiner 2: 1 question about the talk | answers each question; in round B gives feedback + asks 1 question about the partner's talk |

The weakness to beat: TestGerman's B1 "partner" plays six fixed lines and ignores what the learner said. Teile 2–3 are missing entirely (see §6.4). A partner that reacts to the candidate's last turn, at least in Teil 1 and in the Teil 3 question, is the realism gap.

## 6. Kurztest composition proposal

The pattern follows `lib/exam/blueprint.ts` (`KURZTEST`): 10 items per closed skill, every Teil represented, real block structure kept (one text or clip carries its items), and the real answer formats and play counts. Real per-Teil shares: Lesen 6/6/7/7/4 (= 20/20/23/23/13%); Hören 10/5/7/8 (= 33/17/23/27%).

### 6.1 Lesen (10 items, ~22 min ≈ 65 × 10/30)

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Real share → Kurztest | How to cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 personal email/blog (full length ~300 words) | 2 · R/F | 2.0 → 2 | keep the text full length; pick 2 of the 6 statements |
| 2 | 1 press text (~250 words) | 2 · abc | 2.0 → 2 | use one of the two texts |
| 3 | 1 ad set: 3 situations + ~6 ads | 3 · matching incl. "0" | 2.3 → 3 | fewer ads keeps it phone-sized, but keep **one situation without a match** so the "0" option stays real |
| 4 | 1 forum page: 2 comments + example | 2 · Ja/Nein | 2.3 → 2 | keep the question header and the name/age/city signature format |
| 5 | 1 Hausordnung/Anleitung (~250 words) | 1 · abc | 1.3 → 1 | full text, 1 item |

### 6.2 Hören (10 items)

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Real share → Kurztest | Plays | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 short texts (e.g. 1 voicemail + 1 Durchsage) | 4 = 2 × (R/F + abc) | 3.3 → 4 | **2×** | Teil 1 items come in pairs, so 4 is the nearest valid count |
| 2 | 1 monologue | 2 · abc | 1.7 → 2 | **1×** | |
| 3 | 1 dialogue (m+f) | 2 · R/F | 2.3 → 2 | **1×** | |
| 4 | 1 radio discussion, **3 voices** (moderator + 2 guests) | 2 · match to a/b/c speaker | 2.7 → 2 | **2×** | needs 3 distinct cast voices |

**Trade-off for Hannes.** The full Teil 2–4 clips run ~3–5.5 min each, which means ~25 min of audio for 6 items. Options:

- **(a)** Keep real-length clips. This is the most realistic but heavy on mobile data and time.
- **(b)** Use shorter clips (~60–90 s) that keep the real text type, speaker setup and play count.

The DTZ Kurztest used real-length clips. **TODO(hannes):** choose (a) or (b); (b) needs an on-page note that clips are shorter than in the exam.

### 6.3 Schreiben (1 task): **Aufgabe 1, informal email, ca. 80 words, 20 min**

Why Aufgabe 1:

- It carries 40 of 100 points, as much as Aufgabe 2 and twice Aufgabe 3.
- It exercises all four criteria plus register and the required text structure.
- The three fixed Leitpunkte (beschreiben / begründen / Vorschlag machen) make "Erfüllung" checkable per point, so rubric grading is most reliable here.
- 20 min fits a lead-magnet session.

Alternative: Aufgabe 2 (opinion), the task candidates find least familiar from the DTZ. Grade on the official 4 criteria × A–E (10/7.5/5/2.5/0), including the "Erfüllung E → 0" rule and the < 50% word-count rule.

### 6.4 Sprechen

- **Teil 1:** full planning card with 4 Leitpunkte and a TTS partner (5–6 partner turns), ~3 min.
- **Teil 2:** choice of 2 topics, 5 slides, ~3 min, with preparation time shown. Recommended: a 5-min timer for Teile 1+2 together in the Kurztest, labelled as shorter than the real 15 min. **TODO(hannes):** confirm.
- **Teil 3:** the candidate answers the partner's feedback-question and examiner 2's question. Skip the partner's own presentation (round B) in the Kurztest to save ~4 min. Offer it in the full Modelltest.

Score on the official grid (T1 28 / T2 40 / T3 16 / Aussprache 16). If Teil 3 is shortened, show the indicative total on /100 only.

### 8.8 Generation checklist (what set 1 must hit)

- **Lesen:**
  - T1 one personal text (~330–350 words) with feelings; items in text order; example R.
  - T2 two texts (~200 words each, different regions) with a source line; the first item of each text asks the main idea; example only on text 1.
  - T3 a theme line + 7 one-sentence situations with names + example; 10 ads with their own sender style (domain, phone, address) and mixed regions; one 0, placed mid-list; 3 ads unused, at least one a near-miss for the 0 situation.
  - T4 a question line + framing; 8 signed comments (example + 7), 4–5 D, 1–2 A, 2 CH; 3 Ja / 4 Nein.
  - T5 items before the text; a one-sentence situation ("Sie informieren sich über …, in dem Sie …"); rules text with sub-headings; items not in text order.
  - Gloss one regional word per set, in Lesen 1 or 2.
- **Hören:**
  - T1: example (voicemail between friends) + 5 texts of mixed type, each with its own spoken situation line and sound cue; 66–85 words; 1 speaker.
  - T2: one speaker addressing a group, informal *ihr* or formal *Sie*.
  - T3: two named friends (m + f), with back-channels and hesitations.
  - T4: moderator + 2 guests with opposite views; moderator gets exactly 1 of 8 statements; guests' names and roles in the situation line.
  - Track: the measured pauses above, the narrator's fixed lines, 5-min transfer at the end.
- **Schreiben:** the three fixed frames above; A3 usually an apology with reason, sometimes another polite request.
- **Sprechen:** a planning card with 4 questions + "…"; 2 presentation topics with slide 1 quote + title; the moderation script.
