## 1. telc Deutsch B2 (exam no. 1023)

**Overview** [S-B2-UT p. 5, 32–33; S-B2-WEB]
- **Written exam: 140 min, no break.** Leseverstehen and Sprachbausteine share one 90-min block, then
  Hörverstehen (~20 min), then Schriftlicher Ausdruck (30 min).
  - The S-B2-WEB table shows „Reading 90 min“, with Sprachbausteine listed separately and no time given.
    The Übungstest is explicit that the 90 min cover both subtests.
- **Oral exam: ~15 min, as a pair.** A group of three takes ~25 min (max. one per session). A single
  candidate is possible only if just one person registered, in which case an examiner acts as partner.
  Two licensed examiners run it: one Interlokutor (moderator) and one Assessor.
- **Preparation: 20 min** for speaking Teil 2 and Teil 3, with notes on stamped sheets only.
  Teil 1 is normally prepared at home: 7 topics are published and the candidate picks one.
- **Audio:** every Hören part is played **once**. Playback may not be paused. Total MP3 length of
  Übungstest 1 is **18:28 min** (measured).
- **Writing:** a different writing task number per candidate version. The candidate must copy the
  number onto the answer sheet, or the writing scores 0.

### Leseverstehen (90 min together with Sprachbausteine; 75 pts = 25%)

| Teil | Task / goal | Text type | Length (measured in UT1) | Items | Answer format | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Globalverstehen | Match a heading to each text | 5 short authentic texts from two topic areas: newspaper reports, magazine articles, short info texts | ~950 words incl. 10 headings (≈170–190 words per text) | 5 (1–5) | Matching: 10 headings a–j for 5 texts (5 distractors) | 5 × 5 = 25 |
| 2 Detailverstehen | Detailed understanding of an article | Article of general interest (newspaper, magazine, internet). The handbook says one text; UT1 has two related short articles | ~550 words of text (UT1) | 5 (6–10) | 3-option MC a/b/c | 5 × 5 = 25 |
| 3 Selektives Verstehen | Find the text that fits each situation | 12 ads or short info texts (brochures, info sheets) | ~1,100 words incl. 10 situations (≈70–90 words per ad) | 10 (11–20) | Matching 10 situations to 12 texts a–l. Each text used at most once; **„x“ = no matching text** is a valid answer | 10 × 2.5 = 25 |

### Sprachbausteine (inside the 90 min; 30 pts = 10%)

| Teil | Focus | Text type | Length | Items | Format | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Grammatik | Fill gaps, grammar focus | Semi-formal or informal letter/e-mail | ~350 words incl. options | 10 (21–30) | 3-option MC per gap | 10 × 1.5 = 15 |
| 2 Lexik | Fill gaps, vocabulary focus | Simple newspaper/magazine article of general interest | ~385 words incl. word box | 10 (31–40) | **Word pool: 15 words a–o for 10 gaps**, each used once, 5 distractors | 10 × 1.5 = 15 |

### Hörverstehen (~20 min; 75 pts = 25%)

| Teil | Goal | Text type | Reading time before | Items | Format | Plays | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Global | Global understanding | Radio news bulletin with 6 short news items after a brief intro | 30 s | 5 (41–45) | Richtig/Falsch (+/–) | **once** | 5 × 5 = 25 |
| 2 Detail | Detail | Radio interview or conversation with a narrative feel, on a general-interest topic | 60 s | 10 (46–55) | Richtig/Falsch | **once** | 10 × 2.5 = 25 |
| 3 Selektiv | Key details | 5 short authentic texts: announcements, radio ads, voicemails, each with a short context intro | – | 5 (56–60) | Richtig/Falsch | **once** | 5 × 5 = 25 |

- Handbook rule: „falsch“ means contradicted by the text, never „not in the text“. B2 has no third
  option „nicht im Text“.
- Transcript length in UT1 is ~2,650 words in total. The columns are interleaved, so the per-part split is
  UNVERIFIED. Roughly, Teil 2 is the longest and Teil 3 the shortest.

### Schriftlicher Ausdruck (30 min; 45 pts = 15%)

- **One task.** The candidate chooses Aufgabe A or B: **a semi-formal e-mail** to a company, person or office.
  In UT1: A = Bitte um Informationen, B = Beschwerde. The input is a short ad or situation (~230–290 words
  including the instructions).
- Cover either **3 of 4 Leitpunkte**, or 2 Leitpunkte plus one aspect of your own. The candidate chooses a
  sensible order, a subject line, a greeting, an introduction and a closing.
- **At least 150 words.** No letter-form features are required (sender, recipient, date).
- **Criteria** [S-B2-UT p. 42–45], each rated A/B/C/D = **5/3/1/0**. The raw total (max 15) **× 3 = 45**.
  1. **Aufgabenbewältigung** (content). Each Leitpunkt needs more than one sentence structure.
     - „Thema verfehlt“ = D in all criteria.
     - „Situierung verfehlt“ = D in criterion I only.
  2. **Kommunikative Gestaltung** (cohesion, coherence, register, range of vocabulary)
  3. **Formale Richtigkeit**
- Two licensed raters score each text. The second rating overrides the first.

### Mündlicher Ausdruck (~15 min, pair; 75 pts = 25%)

| Teil | Time | What happens | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| (Einander kennenlernen) | ~1 min | Short introduction to each other. **Not scored.** | – |
| 1 Über Erfahrungen sprechen | ~5 min | Each candidate speaks ~1.5 min on the topic they chose from 7 (book, film, trip, event, a person, an experience…). The partner listens, then asks follow-up questions. Roles swap. Memorised speeches are interrupted by examiners. | 25 |
| 2 Diskussion | ~5 min | Both read the same short controversial text (~280 words in UT1) during preparation. Each sums up its points briefly (max. 1 min), then they discuss, bring in their own experience and opinions, and propose solutions. | 25 |
| 3 Gemeinsam etwas planen | ~5 min | Jointly organise a given project (event, party, trip…): make proposals, react to the partner's, agree a plan. | 25 |

**Criteria (applied to each Teil separately)** [S-B2-UT p. 40–41]:

| Criterion | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ausdrucksfähigkeit | 7 | 5 | 3 | 0 |
| Aufgabenbewältigung | 7 | 5 | 3 | 0 |
| Formale Richtigkeit | 7 | 5 | 3 | 0 |
| Aussprache und Intonation | 4 | 2 | 1 | 0 |

That gives max. 25 per Teil and 75 in total. Both examiners score independently, then agree a joint rating.

### Pass rule, grades, retakes (B2)

- **Total 300** = written 225 (75%) + oral 75 (25%).
- **Pass requires 60% in each part:** ≥135 written **and** ≥45 oral. No per-subtest minimum.
- **Grades:**

  | Points | Grade |
  |---|---|
  | 270–300 | sehr gut |
  | 240–269.5 | gut |
  | 210–239.5 | befriedigend |
  | 180–209.5 | ausreichend |
  | <180 | nicht bestanden |

  [S-B2-UT p. 46]
- **Partial retake:** the failed or missed part (written OR oral) can be retaken alone.
  - **The current rule (S-REG25, rule on repeating exams, no. 3, valid from 15.04.2025) is within 12 months** of the first
    result sheet, and must be requested at registration. Individual subtests cannot be retaken. The
    latest result counts.
  - Older Übungstest editions say „within the same or the following calendar year“, which is outdated.
  - B1, B2, C1 and C2 allow partial crediting; A1, A2 and two-level exams (A2·B1, B1·B2, B2·C1) do not
    [S-REG25 Anhang 1].
- **Delivery:** paper, hybrid or digital [S-B2-WEB; S-LIST].
  - **Hybrid** = written on paper + speaking via conference software in a virtual exam room.
  - **Digital** = written on a computer + the same online speaking.
  - Content is identical across modes [S-DIGI].
  - Digital exam numbers start with 8, e.g. 1061 → 8061 [S-REG25]. By that scheme digital B2 would be
    8023, which is not stated explicitly (UNVERIFIED).
- **Format revisions 2024–2026:** none found.
  - The product page and the handbook (2019) show the same structure as Übungstest 1 (2021 printing).
  - No „neues Format“ notice on telc.net. UNVERIFIED whether telc plans a revision; nothing was published
    as of 2026-10-23.
