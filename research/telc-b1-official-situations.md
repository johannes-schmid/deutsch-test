# telc Deutsch B1: situations in the official Übungstests (do-not-reuse list)

**Stand: 23.10.2026** · paraphrased from telc Übungstest 1 Zertifikat Deutsch (13. Auflage 2020) and its MP3 (`telc_deutsch_b1.zip`, [S1]/[S3] in `telc-b1.md`).

generator can avoid them (`scripts/modelltest/author-telc-b1.ts`). The official wording for the 6-word overlap check lives

--- Lesen Teil 1
- Leisure research: who uses the home computer most (young men with higher education; word processing and games on top).
- Rail brochure for cycling holidays: take the train, rent a bike on site; price of the brochure.
- Patient guidebook on sleep problems sold in pharmacies.
- Family saver fare on trains between Austria and Germany from a date in October; who counts as a family.
- Study on cultural interest of tourists by age group and income.
- Distractor headlines: families travelling by train, cheap sleeping pills, women and computers, tourists going west, cycling holidays more popular.
=====

--- Lesen Teil 2
- Article: an office workshop in Vienna trains young people with physical disabilities on computers for five months; firms buy their services; legal quota of one disabled employee per 25 staff or a monthly levy; a third found jobs since the association was founded; founded by a teacher.
=====

--- Lesen Teil 3
- Situations: eat outside in a restaurant garden; buy a hot meal to take away; fly to the USA with accommodation; last-minute flight; cheap student flight to the USA; maths tutoring for a son; speech therapy for a friend's child; German course for a young French visitor; youth club for a son (no ad); interest in problems of foreign youths (discussion evening).
- Ads: Asian restaurant with take-away and delivery; Italian restaurant with garden; insurance office holiday greeting; flight and hotel specialist with USA summer dates; student travel agency price list; pub with beer garden and live football; international discussion evening on German and foreign youths; camper rental USA/Canada; two maths/physics tutoring ads; courses for people with speech difficulties; language school with German as a foreign language.
=====

--- Sprachbausteine Teil 1
- Informal letter to a friend after an internship in France: wanted Paris, ended up at a TV manufacturer in Strasbourg for three months, hard work but liked it, nice colleagues, two weeks holiday with friends afterwards.
=====

--- Sprachbausteine Teil 2
- Family enquiry to a hotel/guesthouse owner in Austria after reading an ad: holiday dates in August, children's leisure programme, swimming and tennis courses for children and their price, discount for children per week, may we bring our small dog, please send brochures.
=====

--- Hören Teil 1
- Radio survey on sharing housework between married partners; five people say who cooks, washes up, does laundry, shops, gardens (one wife does everything, one husband cooks and shops, one stay-at-home mother does almost all, one couple splits by situation, one lazy husband).
=====

--- Hören Teil 2
- Radio interview with the secretary (Schriftführerin) of a town sports club turning 100: old papers survived, a festschrift printed the first programme, women joined after 16 years, today over half the members are women, outdoor grounds and a hall, half the money from membership fees, the rest from the town and the sports federation, every sport except swimming (a swimming club next door), trainers trained in the club.
=====

--- Hören Teil 3
- Directions on the phone from the station to an office opposite a cinema (which street).
- Cinema programme of a city's film theatres (which film in which cinema).
- Weather report for southern and northern Bavaria (afternoon sunny, up to 18 degrees).
- ICE welcome announcement: dining car opening time, minibar sells snacks and drinks (not newspapers).
- Department store summer sale: skirts, pullovers, shirts and trousers with prices.
=====

--- Schreiben
- A friend accepts your invitation to visit your home country and asks about the best time, the heat, and what to know before the trip. Points: excursions together, best season, clothes to bring, how to prepare.
=====

--- Sprechen Teil 1
- Getting to know each other: name, origin, housing, family, where German was learned, school/study/job, languages. Extra examiner topics: weekend, hobbies.
=====

--- Sprechen Teil 2
- Topic "group travel": one sheet for travelling in a group (company, guide), one against (fixed programme, prefers to travel alone).
=====

--- Sprechen Teil 3
- Plan a farewell party at the end of a two-week holiday with new German acquaintances: when, where, food, drinks, who pays.
