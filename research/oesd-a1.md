| | |
|---|---|

| **Level** | A1 (GER/CEFR) |
| **Provider** | Österreichisches Sprachdiplom Deutsch (ÖSD), Wien |

## 1. Merge verdict

**Verdict: separate.** ZA1 does **not** share a format with Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 / telc Deutsch A1. Goethe and telc use one joint format with each other, but ÖSD does not.

| | ÖSD ZA1 | Start Deutsch 1 (Goethe / telc) |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | 2 modules (written / oral), **each can be taken and certified separately** [S1][S3] | One exam; telc says it **cannot be taken as a partial exam** [S9] |
| Hören | ca. 10 min · 3 Aufgaben · 15 items: photo matching, **note-taking (played twice)**, interview matrix [S1] | ca. 20 min · 3 Teile · 15 items, a/b/c and richtig/falsch [S9][S10] |
| Lesen | 25 min · 3 Aufgaben · **16 items**: situations to ads, Ja/Nein, notices to pictures [S1] | 3 Teile · 15 items (5+5+5), richtig/falsch and a/b [S9][S10] |
| Schreiben | 20 min · form (10 items) + email (~30 words, 3 questions) [S1] | 2 Teile: form + short message. **This is the closest match.** [S9][S10] |
| Sprechen | **Individual exam** with 2 examiners. Introduce yourself (4 of 6 topics) → describe a photo → role-play the photo situation with the examiner. 10 min preparation. [S1][S3] | **Group exam** (usually 4 candidates), 3 Teile, no preparation time [S9] |
| Scoring | Written module 75 pts (Lesen 30 / Hören 30 / Schreiben 15), oral 25. Minimum per subtest (see §4) [S4] | 60 points of 100 overall [S10] |

The only overlap is the writing section (form + ~30-word personal email) and the self-introduction idea in speaking. Every Hören and Lesen task type is different, as are speaking (individual + picture + role-play vs. a group) and scoring. A shared mock would fail our "nothing surprises you on exam day" rule (CLAUDE.md §2) for both exams.

**Reusable components (not a merge):** the form-filling and short-email writing UI, the self-introduction topic card, and the picture-matching item type. ÖSD needs picture items in Hören Aufgabe 1 and Lesen Aufgabe 3.

### 1a. Variants and modules

- **ZA1 / Österreich:** a variant for migrants, made for Austria, with topics from Austrian daily and working life. **Task types and exam formats are the same** as ZA1. [S1][S2] One blueprint covers both. Only the topic pool differs.
- **Modules:** "Modul Schriftliche Prüfung" (Lesen + Hören + Schreiben, a group exam) and "Modul Mündliche Prüfung" (Sprechen, individual exam). Each module can be taken alone and repeated as often as needed. A passed module gets a **Modulzertifikat**. Both modules passed at one sitting, or within one year at the same centre, get a **Gesamtzertifikat**. The written module can only be repeated as a whole. [S1][S2][S7]
- **Four separate skill modules** (Lesen/Hören/Schreiben/Sprechen as individual modules) exist only for ÖSD Zertifikat B1 and C2. ZA1 does not have them. [S7]
- `TODO(hannes): unverified. The brief mentions an ÖSD "Kompetenzprüfung". No current official ZA1/ZA2 document uses that term (Prüfungsordnung 2026, Durchführungsbestimmungen 10/2023, Modellsatz). If it refers to something specific (e.g. a former product name), please send the source.`
- **Youth variant ÖSD KID A1:** for ages 10+ [S7]. It is a **different exam**: Lesen 30 min, Hören ca. 15 min, Schreiben 20 min (reply to a friend's email, 3 questions), Sprechen ca. 10 min with only 2 tasks ("Sprich über dich" with 5 topic cards, "Sprich mit mir"), individual exam. [S8] It is out of scope for our adult audience.

### 1b. Who uses ZA1 (Germany vs Austria)

- **Austria, "Deutsch vor Zuzug":** A1 proof is required on first application for the Rot-Weiß-Rot – Karte plus, "Familienangehöriger", and several Niederlassungsbewilligungen. Accepted providers: **ÖSD, Goethe-Institut, telc, ÖIF**. The diploma must not be older than one year. Source: BMI (last update 25 Mar 2026) [S11].
- **Germany, spouse reunification (Ehegattennachzug, A1):** leaflets from German missions list **"ÖSD Zertifikat A1"** among accepted ALTE-standard certificates. Examples: Minsk (Stand Dezember 2025) [S12], Rome/germania.diplo.de (Stand Oktober 2023) [S13]. The visa-issuing mission decides on acceptance in each case [S14].

## 2. Hören (ca. 10 min · 30 points · 15 items)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Match each short text to a photo. **One photo is extra.** | 5 short everyday dialogues (2 speakers each, 2–4 turns) in public/private settings (holiday, travel, shopping …) | **33–45 words, 14–17 s per clip** (corrected 2026-10-24, measured; was "≈ 35–50") | 5 · matching, 6 photos A–F (**picture options**). The candidate writes the text number under the photo. A correct text number counts; spotting the unused photo earns nothing | **once** | 30 s to look at the photos first | S1, S5 |
| Aufgabe 2 | Fill in a note sheet with key information from a recorded message | "Tonbandtext": voicemail / Sprachbox / recorded service; read-aloud speech | **≈ 95–100 words, ~51 s, ~112 wpm, 1 speaker** (corrected 2026-10-24, measured; was "≈ 100–120") | 5 · gap fill on a "Notizen" form. The first line ("Was:") is printed as the example. Then weekday · day of month + time (one line, 1+1) · spelled street name · phone number after a printed mobile prefix | **twice** | 30 s to read first; 20 s after the 2nd play to check | S1, S5 |
| Aufgabe 3 | Five people answer one interview question; tick each person's answer in a grid | Short street interviews (Kurzinterviews), spontaneous speech, slightly regional standard German. The interviewer asks the question once at the start; each answer is announced "Text eins" … "Text fünf" | ≈ 39–54 words, 15–20 s per person (confirmed 2026-10-24) | 5 · closed matrix: 5 rows × 4 columns, **one answer per row** (none or more than one tick = 0) | **once** | 10 s to read first; 60 s at the end to check | S1, S5 |

- Total track length in the 2026 Modellsatz, including instructions, pauses and the repeat: ≈ 3:23 / 3:56 / 4:20 for Aufgaben 1–3 [S5].
- **Speakers are from Austria, Germany and Switzerland**, each in their standard variety. Natural background noise may be used, but must not make understanding harder. [S1]
- Scoring per Aufgabe: 5 correct → 10 points, 4 → 8, 3 → 6, 2 → 4, 1 → 2, 0 → 0 [S1].

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** first a short preview line ("look at the photos / read task X, you have N seconds"). Then a one-line "Situation:" framing the listening ("you now hear …"). It always states the play count ("you hear each text once / twice") and warns when one option is extra. The instructions are on the audio track as well as the sheet.

**TestGerman drift (5 "ÖSD A1" mocks):** Aufgabe 2 is played twice in their mocks, so `mock-exams.md`'s "once" is a summary error. They weight Hören at 25 points instead of 30. Their Aufgabe 1 uses one picture panel image, not 6 separate photo options. Full comparison: §9.4.

## 3. Lesen (25 min · 30 points · 16 items)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Find the right ad for each situation. **One ad is extra.** | 5 one-to-two-sentence situations ("you need / you want …") + 6 newspaper-style ads (jobs, services, shops, leisure, phone tariffs …) | ≈ 20–35 words per ad, ≈ 170 words of ads in total | 5 · matching situation → ad no. (1–6) | – | part of 25 min | S1, S5 |
| Aufgabe 2 | Two questions about each of three ads | 3 short ads / info texts (offers, events, flats). In the Modellsatz **one each from DE, AT and CH** (€ and CHF prices, .de/.at/.ch addresses) | ≈ 30–40 words per ad | 6 · **JA / NEIN** (2 per text; two ticks = wrong). Officially "Ja/Nein- bzw. Richtig/Falsch-Format" | – | | S1, S5 |
| Aufgabe 3 | Match each short text to a picture. **One picture is extra.** | 5 public notices/signs (hospital, office, park, restaurant, transport) | ≈ 15–30 words each | 5 · matching text → picture no. (1–6). The pictures are **pictograms and sign icons** (prohibition signs, a bus symbol, one clip-art figure), not photos; the extra one is a near-miss sign | – | | S1, S5 |

- Scoring: Aufgaben 1 and 3: 5 correct → 10 points, then −2 per error. Aufgabe 2: 6 → 10, 5 → 8, 4 → 6, 3 → 4, 2 → 2, 1–0 → 0. [S1]
- Texts are "(semi-)authentic" texts from Austria, Germany and Switzerland. Solution-relevant regional words are explained (e.g. in a footnote). [S1]

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** a "Situation:" line ("you are looking for things in the newspaper / you read three ads / you read five short texts"). Then the action (write the number next to each situation / tick JA or NEIN). Then an explicit "Achtung: one is extra" where it applies.

**TestGerman drift:** their mocks have 5 Lesen parts (they split Aufgabe 2 into three 2-question parts) and weight Lesen at 25 points. Aufgabe 1 is shown as a website, not newspaper ads. Aufgabe 3 uses one picture panel. (Corrected 2026-10-24: their Aufgabe 2 answers **are** Ja/Nein, not R/F.)

## 4. Schreiben (20 min · 15 points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Fill in a form for a friend, using the facts in a short situation | Situation text (≈ 50 words) + form (booking/registration type) | – | **10 items**: fill-in fields + tick boxes (Auswahl- und Fill-in-Format). 10 → 5 pts, 9–8 → 4, 7–6 → 3, 5–4 → 2, 3–2 → 1, 1–0 → 0 | – | part of 20 min | S1, S4 |
| Aufgabe 2 | Reply to a friend's personal email that asks 3 questions | Personal email (informal, du) | **ca. 30 words** | 1 text · answer all 3 Leitfragen + a closing greeting. The sheet gives a reply opener. 10 points | – | | S1, S4 |

**Aufgabe 2 rating criteria** (Auswertungsbogen Vers. 6.0, 28 Jul 2026) [S4]:

- **Textlänge:** ≥ 25 words, no deduction · 24–20 words, −1 · 19–15 words, −2 · < 15 words, whole task not passed (0)
- **Aufgabe (content):** all questions answered, 0 · 2 of 3 answered properly, −1 · only 1, −2 · off topic, task scores 0
- **Kommunikative Angemessenheit / Textsorte** (greeting formula, reference to the input email, register, tone): 1 / 0
- **Textaufbau / Kohärenz** (simple connectors like "und", "dann"): 1 / 0
- **Lexik:** 4 / 3 / 2–1 / 0
- **Formale Richtigkeit** (morphology, syntax, spelling): 4 / 3 / 2–1 / 0
- Deductions never take the result below 0.
- Form fields (Aufgabe 1): content must match the situation. Spelling counts for names, but not for words whose meaning is clear. Any common German date format is accepted. [S2]

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** a "Situation:" paragraph sets up who you are helping or answering. Then a bullet list: write about N words, answer all questions (listed), add a greeting at the end.

**TestGerman drift:** their forms have 7–9 fields (real: 10 items). The email is "formal or informal" with 5 AI content points (real: informal, 3 questions + greeting). Writing is weighted 25 points (real: 15).

## 5. Sprechen (ca. 10 min · 25 points · individual exam)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Introduce yourself: **pick 4 of 6 topics** and say a few sentences on each. The examiner may then ask follow-up questions | Topic card with 6 keywords (job, languages, family, hobbies, sport, favourite food …) | "a few sentences" per topic | Mostly monologue + follow-up questions | – | Within ca. 10 min total | S1, S2, S6 |
| Aufgabe 2 | **Pick 1 of 3 photos** and describe it along 4 simple questions (what do you see, how many people, where are they, what are they doing) | Photo of an everyday situation (e.g. shop, ticket office, doctor) | – | Mostly monologue. The examiner then asks questions about the photo | – | | S1, S2, S6 |
| Aufgabe 3 | **Role-play the situation in the chosen photo** with the examiner | Same photo as Aufgabe 2 | – | Free dialogue. Turn-taking, register and du/Sie matter | – | | S1, S2, S6 |

**Preparation:** 10 minutes alone before the exam, for all three tasks. Notes are made on stamped scrap paper. The candidate picks the photo for Aufgaben 2+3 from three pre-selected ones. [S1][S2]

### Who does what (for our TTS simulation)

- **Two examiners.** The **Assessor/-in** leads, explains each task briefly and takes notes. The **Interlokutor/-in** is the conversation partner. [S2]
- **Warm-up:** greeting, examiners introduce themselves, ID check, then a short **unscored** small talk (~1 min). [S2]
- **Aufgabe 1:** the candidate talks. Then the Interlokutor asks **follow-up questions about the person**. [S2]
- **Aufgabe 2:** the candidate describes the photo. Then the Interlokutor asks **questions about the chosen photo**. [S2]
- **Aufgabe 3:** the Interlokutor **plays the other role** in the photo situation (e.g. sales assistant, ticket clerk) and interacts with the candidate. [S2]
- The exam is recorded. Both examiners rate independently, then agree on a score. [S2]

For us this means one TTS "Interlokutor" voice that:

1. asks 1–2 follow-up questions after Aufgabe 1,
2. asks 1–2 questions after Aufgabe 2,
3. plays a scripted role with branching turns in Aufgabe 3.

A separate "Assessor" voice reads the task explanations. There is no second candidate at A1.

## 6. Scoring and pass rule

Official Gesamtbogen Vers. 3.0 (5 Jun 2026) [S4]:

| Module | Max | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 30 | min. 6 |
| Hören | 30 | min. 6 |
| Schreiben 1 + 2 | 15 (5 + 10) | min. 4 |
| **Written module** | **75** | **min. 38**, and every subtest minimum met |
| **Oral module (Sprechen)** | **25** | **min. 12** |
| Overall (optional) | 100 | min. 50, and all minimums met |

- **Grades:**
  - Written: 75–66 sehr gut, 65–56 gut, 55–38 bestanden, 37–0 nicht bestanden
  - Oral: 25–22 sehr gut, 21–19 gut, 18–12 bestanden, 11–0 nicht bestanden
  - Overall: 100–88 sehr gut, 87–75 gut, 74–50 bestanden, 49–0 nicht bestanden [S4][S1]
- **Not "50% per section"** (the TestGerman claim). The real rule is about 50% per **module**, plus low subtest floors (20% in Lesen and Hören, 4/15 in Schreiben).
- No dictionary or electronic devices are allowed. [S1]

## 7. Kurztest composition (proposal)

This mirrors the DTZ cut in `lib/exam/blueprint.ts`: about 10 items per closed skill, every Teil in real proportion, every Teil represented, and the "one option too many" rule kept in every matching block.

**Lesen: 10 items (real 5 / 6 / 5 = 16)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 situations + **4 ads** (keep 1 extra) |
| Aufgabe 2 | 2 | 2 each = 4 | 2 ads × 2 JA/NEIN questions |
| Aufgabe 3 | 1 | 3 | 3 notices + **4 pictures** (keep 1 extra) |

**Hören: 10 items (real 5 / 5 / 5 = 15)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 short dialogues + **4 photos**, played once, 30 s preview |
| Aufgabe 2 | 1 | 4 | 1 voicemail, 4-gap note sheet, **played twice** |
| Aufgabe 3 | 1 | 3 | 3 interviewees × 4-column grid, played once |

**Schreiben:** Aufgabe 2 (reply to a friend's email, ~30 words, 3 questions + greeting), rated on the S4 criteria. Optional and cheap: a 5-field mini form (Aufgabe 1) scored by rules, with no model call.

**Sprechen:** all three Aufgaben in order: intro (4 of 6 topics) → photo (1 of 3) → role-play of that photo with the TTS Interlokutor. Include a short 2–3 min version of the 10-min preparation step.

### 9.3 Generation checklist (set 1)

- **Lesen A1:** 2-sentence situations; 6 newspaper-style ads, 20–30 words each, mixed regions (≈ 2 D, 2–3 A, 1 CH); the extra ad shares keywords with 2–3 situations but serves another purpose.
- **Lesen A2:** three texts, one each D / A / CH (€/CHF, .de/.at/.ch); 2 JA/NEIN per text; JA:NEIN = 2:4 in the MS.
- **Lesen A3:** 5 notices addressed to a group ("Liebe …!"); 6 pictograms; the extra pictogram is a near-miss sign.
- **Hören A1:** 5 two-person dialogues, 33–45 words, each opened by "Text n." and a 2–4 s sound cue; 6 photos, the extra one a plausible scene.
- **Hören A2:** one voicemail, ~95–100 words at ~110–120 wpm; "Was:" pre-filled; weekday / date + time / spelled street / mobile number after a printed prefix; distractor numbers and days.
- **Hören A3:** interviewer asks once; 5 answers (f, m, f, m, f) of 40–55 words; each speaker names 2–3 options and settles on one; one column used twice.
- **Track:** gong (xylophone) between every step; previews 30 / 30 / 10 s; beep before/after the voicemail; 20 s and 60 s check times; total ~11.5 min.
- **Schreiben A1:** ~50-word situation; surname pre-filled; 5 written + 5 tick points incl. one 4-box group with 2 right answers.
- **Schreiben A2:** informal input email (~50 words, 3 questions in the body and repeated as bullets); printed opener; instructions say "das E-Mail".
- **Sprechen:** topic card (6 keywords with "?"); 3 two-person service photos; Assessor + Interlokutor script with 3 / 2 / ~8 Interlokutor turns and a role choice.
- **Result:** module points with minima and Prädikat (§6).
