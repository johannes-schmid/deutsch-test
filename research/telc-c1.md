## 1. telc Deutsch C1 (exam no. 1029), format since 2016

C1 general was re-based in 2016 („Format 2016“ in the Übungstest title) onto the **same task architecture
as C1 Hochschule**. Topics are general or cultural rather than academic, and the writing input differs.
[S-C1-UT p. 5]

**Overview**
- **Written:** Lesen + Sprachbausteine 90 min → **20 min break** → Hören ~40 min → Schreiben 70 min.
  That is ≈220 min including the break [S-C1-UT p. 5; S-C1-WEB].
- **Oral: ~16 min, pair** (single or trio possible). **20 min preparation.** Each candidate gets a
  different Teil 1A task sheet (A/B/C). Notes are allowed, talking and dictionaries are not.
  - Rough split: 1A 3–4 min, 1B 2–3 min, Teil 2 ~6 min [S-C1-UT p. 49–50].
- **Audio: every part played once.** Übungstest 1 MP3 = **41:16 min** (measured).
- **Delivery:** the telc exam list tags C1 as „Hybrid, Paper“ [S-LIST], but the C1 product page says
  „digital, hybrid and paper-based“ [S-C1-WEB]. The two conflict, so digital C1 availability is UNVERIFIED.

### Leseverstehen (48 pts = 22.5%)

| Teil | Goal | Text type | Length (UT1, incl. items) | Items | Format | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Textrekonstruktion | Restore removed sentences | Magazine article / short essay | ~550 words incl. 8 sentences | 6 (1–6) + example 0 | Matching: 8 sentences a–h for 6 gaps (2 distractors) | 6 × 2 = 12 |
| 2 Selektives Verstehen | Which paragraph answers which question | Article in 5 paragraphs a–e (UT1: a personal/feature piece) | ~820 words incl. items | 6 (7–12) | Matching question → paragraph a–e. A paragraph can be used more than once | 6 × 2 = 12 |
| 3 Detail + Global | Statements true, false or not in text, plus a best headline | Long non-fiction article | ~1,380 words incl. 11 statements | 11 (13–23) + 1 (24) | **richtig (+) / falsch (–) / nicht im Text (x)**; item 24 = 3-option headline MC | 11 × 2 + 2 = 24 |

### Sprachbausteine (22 pts = 10%)

| Teil | Focus | Text | Length | Items | Format | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (only) | Grammar + vocabulary | Non-fiction text | ~650 words incl. options (handbook HS: text 320–350 words) | 22 (25–46) + example | **4-option MC a/b/c/d** per gap | 22 × 1 = 22 |

### Hörverstehen (~40 min; 48 pts = 22.5%)

| Teil | Goal | Text type | Prep | Items | Format | Plays | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Global | Match an opinion to each speaker | 8 short opinion statements on one topic (each ~30–45 s, ~100–140 words) | 1 min | 8 (47–54) | Matching from **10 statements a–j** (2 distractors) | once | 8 × 1 = 8 |
| 2 Detail | Detail in a long broadcast | Radio programme / interview (~1,100 words) | 3 min | 10 (55–64) | 3-option MC | once | 10 × 2 = 20 |
| 3 Informationstransfer | Note-taking | Lecture/talk (~10 min, ~1,100 words). The candidate gets „handout slides“ with gaps | 1 min, plus **5 min transfer time** afterwards | 10 (65–74) | **Short answers in note form** (open, rated) | once | 10 × 2 = 20 |

Transcripts in UT1 total ≈3,700 words. Lengths per part are from the C1 Hochschule handbook
(same specification) [S-HS-HB p. 36–41].

### Schriftlicher Ausdruck (70 min; 48 pts = 22.5%)

- **Choose 1 of 2 topics.** Write an argumentative text: Erörterung or Stellungnahme. Set out pros and
  cons and justify your own position. **About 350 words** („etwa 350“) [S-C1-UT p. 20].
- The input is a situation (e.g. a local newspaper's „question of the week“, a statement for a school
  newsletter) plus two contrasting quoted opinions as prompts.
- **Criteria**, each rated A/B/C/D = **12/8/4/0** (max 48):
  1. Aufgabengerechtheit
  2. Korrektheit
  3. Repertoire
  4. Kommunikative Gestaltung
- „Thema verfehlt“ = D in all four. Rater 2 overrides rater 1 [S-C1-UT p. 38–41].

### Mündlicher Ausdruck (~16 min, pair; 48 pts = 22.5%)

| Teil | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| (warm-up) | short | Getting to know each other (not separately scored) |
| 1A Präsentation | ~3 min each | Each candidate presents **1 of 2 topics** on their own sheet as a short talk (e.g. „in a cultural club“). Needs an introduction, a structure, examples and a conclusion. Notes allowed as keywords only. |
| 1B Zusammenfassung + Anschlussfragen | ~2 min each | The partner summarises what was notable in the talk (not a repeat of its conclusion) and asks **at least one follow-up question**. Examiners may also ask. Then roles swap. |
| 2 Diskussion | ~6 min | Both discuss one provocative statement. The sheet asks: how do you understand it, how far do you agree, give reasons and examples, respond to the partner. Examiners step in if the level drops. |

**Scoring** [S-C1-UT p. 42–46]:
- **Aufgabengerechtheit, per Teil:**

  | Teil | A | B | C | D | Max |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1A | 6 | 4 | 2 | 0 | 6 |
  | 1B | 4 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 4 |
  | 2 | 6 | 4 | 2 | 0 | 6 |

  That is 16 in total.
- **Sprachliche Angemessenheit, rated once for the whole exam:**

  | Criterion | A | B | C | D |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Flüssigkeit | 8 | 5 | 2 | 0 |
  | Repertoire | 8 | 5 | 2 | 0 |
  | Grammatische Richtigkeit | 8 | 5 | 2 | 0 |
  | Aussprache und Intonation | 8 | 5 | 2 | 0 |

  That is 32 in total.
- **Oral max 48.**

### Pass rule, grades, retakes (C1)

- **Total 214** = written 166 (77.5%) + oral 48 (22.5%).
- **Pass:** ≥128 in total **and** ≥60% in each part, i.e. **≥99 written and ≥29 oral**.
- **Grades:**

  | Points | Grade |
  |---|---|
  | 193–214 | sehr gut |
  | 172–192.5 | gut |
  | 151–171.5 | befriedigend |
  | 128–150.5 | ausreichend |
  | 0–127 | nicht bestanden |

  [S-C1-UT p. 47]
- **Partial retake:** written or oral alone. Current rule: within 12 months [S-REG25].
- **Format revisions 2024–2026:** none found. Latest format = 2016.

## 2. telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule (exam no. 1032)

**Purpose:** university entrance. Recognised by HRK and KMK. Under the Rahmenordnung, holders are exempt
from the DSH / Nachweis der sprachlichen Studierfähigkeit [S-HS-UT back cover].

**Structure:** identical to C1 general (same subtests, item counts, timings, points, weighting, pass rule
and grades) [S-HS-UT p. 5, 46–47]. Differences:

| Aspect | C1 Hochschule | C1 general |
|---|---|---|
| Topics | (Popular-)scientific, academic and student-life topics. Hören HV2 is an interview on an academic topic. HV3 is a lecture („Vorlesung/Vortrag“) framed in a university or educational context [S-HS-HB p. 36–41] | General, cultural and societal |
| Writing | „In einer Seminararbeit …“: argue a topic from several perspectives, with two quotes as prompts. **At least 350 words** („mindestens“), structured into Einleitung, Hauptteil and Schluss. Expected genre: Erörterung or Stellungnahme, a factual text possibly with essayistic traits. Input ~45–55 words [S-HS-UT p. 20; S-HS-HB] | „etwa 350 Wörter“, newspaper or school-newsletter framing |
| Speaking 1A | „Presentation in a university course“: 2 topics to choose from, e.g. describe the university system of a country [S-HS-UT p. 22] | „Kurzreferat in a cultural club“ |
| Speaking 2 | 4 statement sheets per version, 1 used, often a quote with attribution | Single statement |
| Delivery | Hybrid, Paper, **Digital** [S-LIST; S-HS-WEB]. Universität Regensburg runs it on paper (third-party, not verified further) | Hybrid, Paper (conflict, see §2) |

**Official length specs** [S-HS-HB 2015, „Lernziele und Testspezifikationen“]:

| Part | Spec length | Measured in UT1 (incl. items) |
|---|---|---|
| LV1 | 400–500 words incl. items; items 9–16 words | ~470 |
| LV2 | 650–850 words | ~820 |
| LV3 | 1,000–1,200 words; items 8–16 words | ~1,220 |
| Reading total | ≈2,200 words | |
| Sprachbausteine | 320–350 words; options 1–4 words | |
| HV1 | 8 × 100–140 words (30–45 s each); native speakers, light accent or dialect possible, (young) adults | |
| HV2 | Interview ~1,100 words, 2–3 speakers (moderator + 1–2 guests) | |
| HV3 | Monologue ~10 min ≈ 1,100 words; 5–7 „slides“ with 3–6 words each | |

Audio MP3 of UT1 = **40:32 min** (measured).

**Scoring, pass rule, grades and retakes:** identical to C1 general (214 / ≥128 / ≥99 written / ≥29 oral;
same grade bands). The criteria names are identical too: writing = Aufgabengerechtheit, Korrektheit,
Repertoire, Kommunikative Gestaltung; speaking = Aufgabengerechtheit + 4 language criteria [S-HS-UT p. 38–47].

- Discrepancy: the 2015 handbook gives LV3 as „24 points“ (11 items × 2 plus the global item).
  The Übungstest shows 22 + 2 for item 24, which is consistent.
- **Format revisions 2024–2026:** none found. The latest Übungstest 1 edition is 2018 and the
  handbook is 2015.
