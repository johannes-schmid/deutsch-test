# Start Deutsch 1 (A1): format blueprint

**Exams covered:** Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 (Goethe-Institut) and Start Deutsch 1 / telc Deutsch A1 (telc gGmbH, exam no. 1070).
**Level:** A1 (GER). **Stand:** 10/2026, all sources checked 2026-10-12.

> Format only. Official Übungssätze and Übungstests are copyright. Nothing here may be reproduced as content (CLAUDE.md §4). Instruction patterns below are paraphrased.

> **Don't confuse with telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer** (exam no. 1069). That is a different exam with a different structure: Sprachbausteine, 2 Hören parts, "Hören und Antworten", Lesen 3 parts in 30 min and Schreiben 2 parts in 15 min ([telc page](https://www.telc.net/sprachpruefungen/zertifikatspruefung/deutsch/telc-deutsch-a1-fuer-zuwanderer/), checked 2026-10-12). It is out of scope for this file. The telc shop PDF `5069-B00-010104` is that exam's Übungstest, not the Start Deutsch 1 one.

## 2. Merge verdict: **merge OK with noted differences**

**Evidence that the format is identical**
- Goethe's Testbeschreibung says Goethe-Institut and telc GmbH developed the exam jointly [G-TB p.5 and the "Qualitätskriterien" section]. The telc Übungstest says both institutes run telc Deutsch A1 (Start Deutsch 1) jointly [T-ÜT p.2].
- **Same parts, item counts, answer formats and play counts.** Compare Goethe Übungssatz 01/02 (Feb 2024) with telc Übungstest 1 (2017):
  - Hören 6 + 4 + 5
  - Lesen 5 + 5 + 5
  - Schreiben: form with 5 fields + a ~30-word message
  - Sprechen: 3 parts
  - Hören Teil 1 and 3 are played twice, Teil 2 once, in both.
- **Same item bank.** Several example items and practice items appear almost unchanged in the Goethe documents and in the telc Übungstest (Lesen Teil 2 example and item 6; Lesen Teil 3 example; Hören Teil 2 example and items). They come from one shared pool. We only note the overlap and never reuse those items.
- **Same rubrics.** Schreiben Teil 2 uses the same scheme: 3 / 1.5 / 0 per content point plus 1 / 0.5 / 0 for text-type conventions, max. 10. Sprechen uses the same full / half / zero rule and the same Teil maxima of 3 / 6 / 6 [G-TB p.37–38, 44–45; T-ÜT p.34–35].

**Differences: none affect the candidate's tasks. All are about administration or how the result is reported.**

| # | Area | Goethe | telc | Impact on our test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reported score | Raw points × 1.66, rounded, out of **100** (75 written + 25 oral) [G-DB §6.1, §6.3] | Raw points out of **60** (15 per skill) [T-ÜT p.5] | Show raw /60 and the /100 equivalent. 36/60 = 60/100 on both. |
| 2 | Pass label | "bestanden" at ≥60 points; below that "nicht bestanden" [G-DB §6.2–6.3] | ≥36 = "ausreichend" or better; 0–35.5 = "teilgenommen" [T-ÜT p.5] | Same 60% threshold. Wording differs. |
| 3 | Grade bands | 90–100 sehr gut, 80–89 gut, 70–79 befriedigend, 60–69 ausreichend [G-DB §6.2] | 54–60 sehr gut, 48–53.5 gut, 42–47.5 befriedigend, 36–41.5 ausreichend [T-ÜT p.5] | Same bands at 90/80/70/60%. |
| 4 | Half points | Two raters' scores averaged and rounded to whole points (Schreiben T2, Sprechen) [G-DB §4.3.2, §5] | Bands run in 0.5 steps, so half points stay in the total [T-ÜT p.5] | Edge cases only. Round like Goethe or keep halves like telc; no effect on the format. |
| 5 | Sprechen timing per Teil | ~5 min each [G-DB §3] | T1 ~3, T2 ~4, T3 ~4 min, plus ~1 min intro and ~4 min for the examiners to agree [T-ÜT p.5, p.31] | Both total 15 min for a group of 4. Use per-candidate turns, not Teil minutes. |
| 6 | Examiners | Always 2 (moderator + assessor) [G-DB §3.3] | 1–2 (interlocutor + assessor) [T-PR Anhang 2; T-ÜT p.31] | None for a self-study simulation. |
| 7 | Lesen/Schreiben timing | Lesen 25 min and Schreiben 20 min shown separately, done in any order [G-DB §1.4, §2.2] | One 45-min block (Lesen ~25, Schreiben ~20) [T-ÜT p.13] | Use one 45-min block with suggested split. That fits both. |
| 8 | Answer-sheet transfer | ~5 min within exam time for Hören [G-DB §2.2]; the older Testbeschreibung says ~3 min [G-TB p.27] | Transfer after Hören; included in the recording's run time [T-ÜT p.30] | Not needed online. Goethe's digital version also drops it [G-DB Anhang]. |
| 9 | Delivery | Paper, digital, online; online Sprechen is a single or pair exam [G-DB Anhang 3.10] | Paper only [T-Web] | None. |
| 10 | Source age | Durchführungsbestimmungen Stand 1 Sept 2025, Übungssätze Feb 2024 | Public Übungstest 1 is the 10th edition, **2017**. No newer public telc spec found. | `TODO(hannes)` below. |

**Conclusion:** one shared practice test matches both exams task for task. Report the result as points per skill and an overall percentage, with a line explaining both scales ("Goethe: x/100 · telc: y/60"). Don't say "bestanden".

## 3. Sections

Abbreviations for sources: see §7. "Measured" means measured by us from the official practice material (word counts from the PDF, clip lengths from the telc Übungstest MP3 with ffmpeg silence detection). Treat those values as a guide, not a rule.

### 3.1 Hören (~20 min, 15 items, 15 raw points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Catch a detail (time, price, place, number) | Short everyday conversation between **two people**: on the street, private, school or work | Measured: 10–25 s per clip (telc ÜT1: 10, 12, 13, 17, 18, 25 s) | **6** · a/b/c, **picture-supported** (each option is a picture plus a short verbal label); question stem above the options | **2×** | Whole Hören ~20 min incl. pauses and repeats | G-TB p.27; T-ÜT p.8–9; G-ÜS01 |
| 2 | Understand the instruction in an announcement | Public loudspeaker announcement: airport, supermarket, underground or train station, train | Measured: 15–21 s (telc ÜT1: 15, 18, 19, 21 s) | **4** · Richtig/Falsch, one statement per announcement | **1×** | n/a | G-TB p.27; T-ÜT p.10 |
| 3 | Catch a detail | Answerphone message or phone announcement, partly private and partly official (doctor's practice, company) | Measured: 8–17 s (telc ÜT1: 8, 11, 11, 14, 17 s) | **5** · a/b/c, text options (usually 1–4 words) | **2×** | n/a | G-TB p.27; T-ÜT p.11 |

- **Examples:** each part starts with a worked example (Beispiel) [G-TB p.27]. In both practice sets, the booklet shows an example for Teil 1 and Teil 2 but none for Teil 3 [T-ÜT p.8–11; G-ÜS01].
- **Pauses:** about 15–16 s of silence after each item, measured from the telc ÜT1 audio. The whole recording runs 16:52 min. The audio is never paused [T-ÜT p.30].
- **Speech:** "semi-authentic" texts at natural speed [G-TB p.27].
- **Instruction pattern (paraphrased):** a general intro says there are three parts, you read the task first, then hear the text and tick, and transfer answers at the end. Each Teil has a one-line instruction: "What is right? Tick a, b or c. You hear each text twice." Teil 2 says "Tick Richtig or Falsch. You hear each text once." The play count is always stated in the instruction.

### 3.2 Lesen (~25 min, 15 items, 15 raw points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | True/false on details | **Two** short personal notes or e-mails (message, instruction, invitation) from private or work life | Measured telc ÜT1: ~40 and ~75 words | **5** (split 2+3 or 3+2 over the two texts) · Richtig/Falsch | n/a | Lesen ~25 min in total | G-TB p.32; T-ÜT p.14–15; G-ÜS01 |
| 2 | "Where do you find the information?" | Situation + **two** small ads or web snippets per item (10 ads for 5 situations), shown with a URL/title | Measured: 10–25 words per snippet | **5** · **a or b** (2 options) | n/a | n/a | G-TB p.32, 34; T-ÜT p.16 |
| 3 | True/false on a notice | Very short **sign or notice** with a place label (at the door of the school, at the post office, at the station, at the bus stop, at a restaurant entrance) | Measured: 5–20 words per sign | **5** · Richtig/Falsch, one statement per sign | n/a | n/a | G-TB p.32; T-ÜT p.18–19; G-ÜS01 |

- Every part has a worked example (item 0). No dictionaries [G-TB p.32].
- In the Goethe Übungssätze, Teil 1 and 3 are labelled Richtig/Falsch. In telc ÜT1 they're labelled richtig (+) / falsch (–). The task is the same.
- **Instruction pattern (paraphrased):** "Read the (two) texts and items 1–5. Tick Richtig or Falsch." Teil 2: "Read the texts and items 6–10. Where do you find information? Tick a or b." Teil 3: like Teil 1, items 11–15. The item range is always named.

### 3.3 Schreiben (~20 min, 2 parts, 15 raw points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fill in a form from a short situation text. All the information is in the text | Form: registration, booking, order (e.g. language-school sign-up, holiday booking) | Situation text measured ~50–70 words | **5** fields · short entries (a word, number or date). Fields can include a tick-box choice. 1 point each | n/a | Schreiben ~20 min in total | G-TB p.37; G-DB §4.3.1; T-ÜT p.20; G-ÜS01/02 |
| 2 | Write a short message covering **3 content points** (Leitpunkte) | Short note or e-mail: an apology, invitation, request or information request (e.g. to a friend or to a tourist office) | **~30 words**; telc asks for "1–2 sentences per point" | **1** task (no choice) · free text, max. 10 points | n/a | n/a | G-TB p.37–38; T-ÜT p.21; G-ÜS02 |

- **Teil 2 scoring:** each content point gets 3 / 1.5 / 0 points (fully done / partly done because of language or content gaps / not done or not understandable). Text-type conventions such as greeting and closing get 1 / 0.5 / 0. Max 3 × 3 + 1 = **10** [G-TB p.37–38; T-ÜT p.34].
- **Spelling** only costs points when it blocks understanding [G-TB p.37]. In Teil 1, misspelled words are accepted, but numbers must be exactly right [T-ÜT p.34].
- **Register:** both informal (to a friend) and semi-formal (to an office) occur in the official practice sets. Address and closing must suit the text type.
- **Instruction pattern (paraphrased):** Teil 1 has a 3–4-sentence situation about a friend, then "Help them and write the five missing pieces of information into the form." Teil 2 has a one-line situation and "Write an e-mail to X:" followed by three bullet prompts (why you write / say … / ask …). The telc version adds "1–2 sentences per point, about 30 words."

### 3.4 Sprechen (~15 min per group, 3 parts, 15 raw points)

| Teil | Task | Prompt material | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce yourself, then **spell** something (name, street, town) and **give a number** (phone, house number, postcode; at least two digits) | Keyword sheet visible to all (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby) | ~80 s per candidate [G-TB p.48] | 3 tasks · 1 point each (introduce / spell / number), max **3** | n/a | Goethe ~5 min; telc ~3 min | G-DB §3.3; G-TB p.48; T-ÜT p.24, 31–32 |
| 2 | Ask for and give information on **2 topics** (e.g. shopping, weekend, food and drink, school, work, sport) | Action cards: topic + one keyword. Each candidate draws **2** (one per topic) | ~60 s per candidate | Per candidate: 2 questions (2 points each) + 2 answers (1 point each), max **6** | n/a | Goethe ~5 min; telc ~4 min | G-TB p.44, 46, 50; T-ÜT p.25, 32–33 |
| 3 | Make a **request** and react to one | Action cards with a pictogram of an everyday object or sign. Each candidate draws **2** | ~60 s per candidate | Per candidate: 2 requests (2 points each) + 2 reactions (1 point each, verbal **or non-verbal**), max **6** | n/a | Goethe ~5 min; telc ~4 min | G-TB p.44, 46, 52; T-ÜT p.26, 33 |

## 4. Scoring and pass rule

| Skill | Raw max | Goethe weighting (×1.66) | telc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hören | 15 (1 per item) | ≈25 | 15 |
| Lesen | 15 (1 per item) | ≈25 | 15 |
| Schreiben | 15 (Teil 1: 5 + Teil 2: 10) | ≈25 | 15 |
| Sprechen | 15 (3 + 6 + 6) | ≈25 | 15 |
| **Total** | **60** | **100** (75 written + 25 oral) | **60** |

Sources: G-DB §4–§6; G-TB p.5, 27, 32, 37, 44; T-ÜT p.5.

- **Pass rule: overall only.** Goethe requires ≥60/100 points and all parts taken [G-DB §6.3]. telc requires ≥36/60 for "ausreichend" [T-ÜT p.5]. **Neither has a per-section minimum.**
- Written and oral are simply added up. Goethe notes that below 35 written points (out of 75) a pass is out of reach, so taking Sprechen makes no sense [G-DB §6.3].
- Bands (both): sehr gut ≥90%, gut ≥80%, befriedigend ≥70%, ausreichend ≥60%.
- **Raters:**
  - Goethe: two independent raters for everything written, Schreiben T2 averaged; two examiners for Sprechen, averaged [G-DB §4–5].
  - telc: one rater, overruled by the central telc rating [T-ÜT p.34]; the two examiners agree a joint score for Sprechen [T-ÜT p.35].
- **Speaking criterion:** understandability counts, not the number of errors. Mild impairment (word stress, syntax, word choice) earns half points; severe impairment earns 0 [G-TB p.45].

## 5. Speaking format in detail

- **Group exam:** up to **4** candidates and **2** examiners [G-DB §1.4, §3.3; G-TB p.44]. telc uses 4, or 3 when the total is odd, with 1–2 examiners [T-PR Anhang 2]. Groups are random [G-DB §3.1]. A lone candidate talks with an examiner in Teil 2 and 3 [G-DB §3.3; G-TB p.44].
- **Roles:** the moderator (Goethe) or interlocutor (telc) gives all instructions, demonstrates each part with an example, steps in when an utterance is unclear, and re-prompts when a candidate asks a question instead of making a request. The second examiner (assessor) scores. Afterwards they agree one score [G-TB p.44–46; T-ÜT p.31–35].
- **No preparation time.** Candidates see the tasks only in the exam [G-DB §3.2].
- **Teil 1 (monologue + two short exchanges):**
  - Candidates introduce themselves one after another, using the keyword sheet. Not every keyword is needed for full points [G-TB p.48].
  - Then the examiner asks each candidate to spell something and to give a number.
  - **Partner role:** the examiner asks these questions (e.g. spell your surname, what's your phone number).
- **Teil 2 (partner Q&A, round robin):**
  - The examiner names topic 1 and models one question with a card. In telc the examiner also models the answer if nobody gives one.
  - Each candidate draws a card and asks the **next candidate** a question on the topic and the card's keyword. That candidate answers, then asks the next one with their own card, and so on round the table.
  - Topic 2 follows the same way. In telc there is no second example.
  - By the end every candidate has produced **2 questions and 2 answers** [G-TB p.46, 50; T-ÜT p.32–33].
  - **Partner role:** another candidate answers your question and asks you one. They use simple A1 W-questions or yes/no questions tied to the card keyword.
  - If a candidate can't form a question, the interlocutor forms it. Off-topic questions are interrupted [T-ÜT p.33].
- **Teil 3 (partner requests, round robin):**
  - Each candidate draws **2** pictogram cards and makes a request or instruction to another candidate (e.g. asking for an object).
  - The partner reacts in words or with a gesture (giving it, or saying politely that they can't).
  - Each candidate makes **2 requests and 2 reactions** [G-TB p.46, 52; T-ÜT p.33].
- **Card sets in practice material:** Teil 2 has 6 cards per topic (telc: 1 for the example + 5). Teil 3 has 12 cards (telc: 1 for the example + 11) [T-ÜT p.32–33; G-ÜS01].
- **Implication for us (CLAUDE.md "partner turns"):**
  - The simulation needs a scripted partner who asks the candidate questions in Teil 2 and makes requests to the candidate in Teil 3.
  - The candidate must also produce questions and requests to that partner.
  - A solo prompt series (TestGerman's approach) misses the 2-point question/request half of the score.

## 6. Kurztest composition (proposal)

Mirrors `lib/exam/blueprint.ts` (`KURZTEST`: 10 items per closed skill, every Teil represented, proportional to the real exam).

**Lesen (real 5 / 5 / 5 → 10 items: 3 / 4 / 3)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items per block | Items | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 | One note or e-mail (~60–75 words), 3 R/F items. The real exam splits 5 items over 2 texts |
| 2 | 4 | 1 | 4 | Each block = situation + 2 snippets, a/b |
| 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | Each block = place label + sign, R/F |

**Hören (real 6 / 4 / 5 → 10 items: 4 / 3 / 3, i.e. 6:4:5 × ⅔ rounded)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items per block | Items | Plays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 1 | 4 | 2× (two voices, picture-supported a/b/c) |
| 2 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1× (one voice, announcement; R/F) |
| 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2× (one voice, phone message; a/b/c) |

**Schreiben:** Teil 2 only (one ~30-word message, 3 Leitpunkte, scored on the 10-point rubric above).
- Optional: add Teil 1 (5 form fields) as a cheap, auto-scorable warm-up. It needs a `form` answer format.

**Sprechen:** all three Teile with a scripted partner (TTS voice from `data/tts-voices.json`):
- **T1:** self-introduction from the keyword sheet, then the examiner asks you to spell one word and say one number.
- **T2:** one topic. You ask the partner 1 question from a drawn card. The partner asks you 1 question and you answer.
- **T3:** you make 1 request from a pictogram card. The partner makes 1 request to you and you react.

**Suggested timers (our proposal, not official):**
- Lesen ~17 min (25 × ⅔).
- Hören runs off the audio, keeping the official pauses (~15 s) and repeats.
- Schreiben T2 ~12 min.

**Gaps in the current kit/blueprint this would need:**
- **Answer format `ab`.** Two options, used in Lesen Teil 2; `AnswerFormat` has no 2-option type yet.
- **Picture options.** Hören Teil 1 uses picture + label options.
- **Answer format `form`.** For Schreiben Teil 1.
- **A place-label slot for signs.** Lesen Teil 3.
- **Partner-initiated turns in Sprechen T2/T3.** The partner asks or requests; the candidate answers or reacts.
- **Picture cards.** Sprechen T3 uses pictogram cards.

Per §8 of CLAUDE.md, each of these goes into the design system and onto `/design/pruefung` first.

## 7. Competitor drift (TestGerman A1 Goethe/telc mocks vs official)

Source: `testgerman/mock-exams.md` › "Goethe A1 / telc A1", `testgerman/a1/pruefungssimulation/{goethe,telc}/`.

- **Correct:** they use one shared template for Goethe and telc. Item counts (6+4+5, 5+5+5), section times, the Lesen T2 a/b format and the 60% overall pass rule all match.
- **Drift:**
  1. **Play counts.** They play every Hören clip **once**. Officially Teil 1 and 3 are played **twice** [G-TB p.27; T-ÜT p.8, 11].
  2. **Picture options.** They have none. Hören Teil 1 is officially picture-supported a/b/c [G-TB p.27].
  3. **Schreiben Teil 1 (form).** It appears only in Goethe mock 1 and is missing from both telc mocks. Officially it's part of every exam at both institutes [G-DB §1.2; T-ÜT p.20].
  4. **Schreiben Teil 2.** They use a 750-character, "formal", 3–6-point task scored by AI. Officially it's ~30 words, 3 content points, plus 1 point for text-type conventions, and it can be informal.
  5. **Sprechen.**
     - They use 12–18 solo prompt cards with 1–2 points each and no partner.
     - Officially there are 3 parts with fixed maxima 3/6/6. Each candidate makes 2 questions + 2 answers and 2 requests + 2 reactions, as a round robin with other candidates.
     - Their Teil 1 splits introduce and spell, and is scored on content points rather than the 1/1/1 introduce/spell/number rule.
  6. **Scoring.** "25 points per section" matches only Goethe's weighted /100 scale. The raw maximum is 15 per skill, and telc reports /60.
