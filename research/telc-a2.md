# telc Deutsch A2 (Start Deutsch 2) — format blueprint

| | |
|---|---|
| **Exam** | telc Deutsch A2, also sold as "Start Deutsch 2" |
| **Level** | CEFR A2 |
| **Provider** | telc gGmbH. The format was originally developed jointly by the Goethe-Institut and telc for the German Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI); telc still states this in its 2024 Übungstest |
| **Audience** | Adults. Separate exams with other formats: telc Deutsch A2 Schule, telc Deutsch A2·B1, telc Deutsch A2+ Beruf |
| **Total duration** | 70 min written (Hören ~20 + Lesen/Schreiben 50) + ~15 min Sprechen (pair) ≈ 85 min, plus 10 min of formalities before the written part |
| **Delivery** | Paper only. No partial exam possible |
| **Stand** | 10/2026, all facts checked 2026-10-12 |

Sources are numbered in §7. Format only. No exam texts, items or audio scripts are reproduced here (CLAUDE.md §4).

## 1. Merge verdict: **do not merge** with Goethe-Zertifikat A2

Companion file: [`goethe-a2.md`](goethe-a2.md) (same verdict and evidence table).

- **Start Deutsch 2 is now only telc's.** Goethe's Übungssatz says the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 **has replaced Start Deutsch 2 since 2015** [G3]. telc keeps selling the old joint format under both names, "Start Deutsch 2 / telc Deutsch A2" [T1][T2].
- **The formats differ structurally:**
  - Sections: 3 + 3 Teile here (15 + 15 items) vs 4 + 4 (20 + 20) at Goethe.
  - Time: one combined 50-minute Lesen + Schreiben block vs separate 30-minute sections.
  - Writing: a form plus a ~40-word letter (choose 3 of 4 points) vs two free texts.
  - Speaking: introduce yourself / question cards / negotiate vs card Q&A / monologue / plan.
  - Scoring: 60 points, pass 36 vs 100 points, pass 60 with 45/75 + 15/25.
  - Hören play counts differ too: telc plays its conversation **twice**, Goethe plays its conversation once [T2][G3].
- **What can be shared:** item-type components only (the store-directory "a / b / anderes Stockwerk" task, a/b/c radio clips, matching with an "x / no match" option, a pair-planning shell). Blueprints, timers and scoring must stay separate.

## 2. Hören (~20 min, 15 items, 15 points)

The duration is set by the recording, which must not be stopped [T2]. Answers go on the Antwortbogen S30.

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete a **phone note**: write the one missing detail (number, time, place, price, day) | Messages and announcements on the phone: answering machine, directory enquiries, timetable info, friends, employer | short monologues (Übungstest ≈ 30–100 words) | 5 · **open gap-fill** (one word/number). Spelling errors are tolerated, but numbers must be exactly right | **2×** | in ~20 | [T2] |
| 2 | Pick the right detail | Five radio items: programme announcement, weather, traffic report, listener greeting, competition | short monologues (≈ 30–100 words) | 5 · a/b/c | **1×** | in ~20 | [T2] |
| 3 | One conversation: match each person to a place / day / thing | Dialogue between two people (e.g. a new intern shown round a company) | ~350 words, 2 voices | 5 · match to options a–i (9 options; the example uses one) | **2×** | in ~20 | [T2] |

Lengths are rough word counts from the Übungstest transcripts [T2], not official specs.

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** "You hear five … / one conversation. There is one task per text / five tasks. Fill in the notes / tick a, b or c / match and write the letter. You hear each text once / twice." Formal *Sie*, with one example (0) per Teil.

## 3. Lesen (in the 50-minute Lesen + Schreiben block, 15 items, 15 points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where do you find X? | Department-store directory by floor | ~75 words (list) | 5 · a / b (named floor) / c "anderes Stockwerk" | – | in 50 | [T2] |
| 2 | Are the statements right or wrong? | Short newspaper/magazine article (people / celebrity news) | ~150 words | 5 · richtig (+) / falsch (–) | – | in 50 | [T2] |
| 3 | Which ad fits which situation? | Eight short ads / website teasers a–h | ~140 words in total (~15–25 per ad) | 5 · letter a–h, **one situation has no match → x** | – | in 50 | [T2] |

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** "Read tasks 1–5 and the information / the text / the ads. Tick a, b or c / right or wrong / Which ad fits which situation? One task has no solution: write x." One example per Teil.

## 4. Schreiben (in the same 50 minutes, 15 points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Help a friend fill in a **form**: take 5 missing details from a short profile text and documents (e.g. an ID card or bank card) | Registration form (e.g. online course sign-up) | 5 short fields | 5 · open fields, 1 point each (right/wrong). Spelling is tolerant; numbers must be exact | – | in 50 | [T2] |
| 2 | Reply to a personal invitation/situation: **choose 3 of 4 prompts**, write 1–2 sentences each, with a suitable opening and closing | Letter / email (the Übungstest answer to an invitation is semi-formal) | **~40 words** | 1 · free text, 10 points | – | in 50 | [T2] |

**Teil 2 criteria** [T2]:
- **Aufgabenerfüllung** per content point: 3 / 1.5 / 0, × 3 points = 9.
- **Kommunikative Gestaltung** (text-type conventions such as salutation and closing): 1 / 0.5 / 0.
- Max 10.

Two licensed raters score. The second rating overrides the first if they differ; telc's own rating is final [T2].

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** a 2-sentence situation, "here are four points, choose three, write one or two sentences about each", "don't forget the opening and the closing greeting", "write about 40 words".

## 5. Sprechen (~15 min per pair, 15 points per candidate)

A **pair exam** in principle; no preparation time. If only one person is registered, one examiner plays the second candidate. Two examiners run it: the *Interlokutor:in* leads, the *Assessor:in* scores. About 4 more minutes go to the examiners' joint decision, so plan 20 min per pair [T1][T2].

| Teil | Task | Format | Materials | Time | Points (per candidate) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sich vorstellen | Introduce yourself to your partner using keyword prompts (name, age, country, home, languages, job, hobby; others allowed). The examiner then asks **2 follow-up questions** per candidate | Pair setting, solo turns + examiner Qs | One keyword sheet on the table for both | ~3 min | Vorstellen 1 + Zusatzfragen 2 = 3 | [T2] |
| 2 Ein Alltagsgespräch führen | On one everyday topic (e.g. daily routine), each draws **3 of 6 question cards** (W-words such as *Wann …? Wo …? Wie oft …?*; each gets one joker "…?"). They take turns: A asks → B answers, B asks → A answers, **3 questions + 3 answers each** | **Pair interaction** | 6 cards (+ 1 example card for the examiner) | ~4 min | 3 questions + 3 answers × 1 / 0.5 / 0 = 6 | [T2] |
| 3 Etwas aushandeln | Agree on joint activities (the Übungstest asks for two): what, when, why / why not | **Pair negotiation**. A and B have **different** mind maps of options, each with a "?" branch for own ideas | Mind-map sheet A ≠ sheet B | ~4 min | Erfüllung der Aufgabenstellung 3 + Sprachliche Realisierung 3 = 6 | [T2] |

**Criteria** (all parts): full points for a task fully completed and understandable; half for partly completed (language/content gaps); 0 for not completed or not understandable. Mutual help between partners counts positively [T2].

**What the partner does (for TTS simulation):**

- **Teil 1:** the partner introduces themselves too (one model intro, ~6 keywords). The **examiner** (a separate TTS voice) asks the candidate 2 follow-ups tied to their intro. Scripted follow-ups per keyword are acceptable for v1.
- **Teil 2:** the most turn-based part, and the easiest to simulate faithfully. The TTS partner asks 3 questions built from its 3 hidden cards on the topic and answers the candidate's 3 questions. Turns alternate strictly, starting with A. If a question goes off-topic, the examiner reminds the candidate of the topic [T2].
- **Teil 3:** a real negotiation. The partner proposes options from **its own** (hidden) mind map, gives reasons (time, money, likes), rejects some and accepts others. It must end with **two** agreed activities and enough detail (when / where). The examiner asks for more precision if the result is vague, and asks for another activity if they agree too fast [T2]. TTS: a branching script with preferences + availability per option.

## 6. Scoring and pass rule

| Subtest | Teile | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Hören | 3 × 5 items | 15 |
| Lesen | 3 × 5 items | 15 |
| Schreiben | T1 5 + T2 10 | 15 |
| Sprechen | 3 + 6 + 6 | 15 |
| **Total** | | **60** |

- **Pass: 60 % of the maximum = 36 points** [T2].
- Grades: 54–60 sehr gut · 48–53.5 gut · 42–47.5 befriedigend · 36–41.5 ausreichend · 0–35.5 teilgenommen [T2].
- A fail means retaking the whole exam (written + oral); no partial credit [T2].

## 7. Kurztest composition (proposal)

This mirrors the DTZ cut in `lib/exam/blueprint.ts`: about 10 items per closed skill, every Teil in real proportion (here 5 : 5 : 5), the real answer formats and play counts, 1 writing task and all speaking parts.

**Hören (10 items)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items/block | Plays | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 phone messages | 1 | 2× | open gap-fill phone note: needs a typed-answer item type with a tolerant spelling check + strict number match |
| 2 | 3 radio items | 1 | 1× | a/b/c |
| 3 | 1 conversation | 4 | 2× | match to 9 options a–i |

**Lesen (10 items)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items/block | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 directory | 3 | a / b / anderes Stockwerk |
| 2 | 1 article | 4 | richtig/falsch |
| 3 | 1 ad set (all 8 ads) | 3 | keep x live: at most one situation without a match |

**Schreiben:** Teil 2 (letter, ~40 words, choose 3 of 4 points). The Teil 1 form can be added as 2–3 auto-scored fields if we want the written block to feel complete. It is cheap and close to the real exam.

**Sprechen:** all three Teile. T1 intro + 1 examiner follow-up. T2: 2 questions asked + 2 answered (2/3 of the real exchange). T3: negotiate one activity (the real task asks for two) against a hidden partner mind map.

The combined Lesen + Schreiben timer is a format feature. A full mock must use **one 50-minute timer** for both, not two section timers.
