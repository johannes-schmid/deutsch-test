# ÖSD Zertifikat A2 (ZA2): format blueprint

| | |
|---|---|
| **Exam** | ÖSD Zertifikat A2 (ZA2). Same rules document: ÖSD Zertifikat A2 / Österreich (migrant variant, offered in Austria) |
| **Level** | A2 (GER/CEFR) |
| **Provider** | Österreichisches Sprachdiplom Deutsch (ÖSD), Wien |
| **Audience** | Learners aged 14+, for everyday and working life. ÖSD says it is official proof "for certain residence purposes, professional qualifications, training" [S3] |
| **Total duration** | Written module ca. 75 min (Lesen 30 + Hören ca. 15 + Schreiben 30), run without a break; a break after Hören is allowed if needed (corrected 2026-10-24, S2 §2). The Modellsatz Hören audio itself runs **10:22** (measured 2026-10-24). Oral module: 10 min preparation + ca. 10 min exam (pair or individual). About 95 min including speaking preparation. [S2][S3] |
| **Modes** | Paper, computer, or hybrid (one module on paper, one on computer). Since 1 Jan 2024 the digital version has the same structure and task formats. [S2][S3] |
| **Stand** | 10/2026, checked 2026-10-12 |

All facts below come from official ÖSD documents (S1–S7), checked 2026-10-12, unless they are labelled otherwise. Lengths marked "≈" were measured by us from the official 2026 Modellsatz. They describe one model set, not a specification.

## 1. Merge verdict

**Verdict: separate.** ZA2 does **not** share a format with Goethe-Zertifikat A2 or Start Deutsch 2 / telc Deutsch A2. It also differs from ÖSD ZA1 (see `research/oesd-a1.md`) in every section except the writing genre.

| | ÖSD ZA2 | Goethe-Zertifikat A2 | Start Deutsch 2 / telc A2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | 2 modules, each can be taken and certified separately [S2][S3] | 4 parts [S9] | One exam, **no partial exam** [S10] |
| Lesen | 30 min · **2 Aufgaben · 10 items** (headings → 5 texts; 1 long text, 5 × A/B/C) [S1][S2] | 30 min · 4 Teile [S9] | 3 Teile, shares 50 min with Schreiben [S10] |
| Hören | ca. 15 min · 3 Aufgaben · **14 items** (4-of-8 keyword pick, note-taking played twice, multi-select interview grid) [S1][S2] | 30 min · 4 Teile [S9] | ca. 20 min · 3 Teile [S10] |
| Schreiben | 30 min · **1** personal email, ~50 words, 4 questions [S1] | 30 min · messages [S9] | 2 Teile [S10] |
| Sprechen | ca. 10 min · **2 Aufgaben** (introduce yourself on 5 of 6 topics; plan something together) · pair **or individual** · 10 min preparation [S2][S3] | 15 min · pair exam · ask/answer, talk about yourself, plan together [S9] | ca. 15 min · 3 Teile · usually 2 candidates · **no preparation** [S10] |
| Scoring | Written 70 (Lesen 25 / Hören 30 / Schreiben 15) + oral 20 = 90. Module and subtest minimums (see §6) [S4] | `TODO(hannes): unverified` | `TODO(hannes): unverified` |

Every closed task type, item count, section timing, the number of speaking tasks and the scoring model are different. Only the genres overlap: "reply to a friend's email" and "plan something together" exist in all three, but with other prompts, lengths and scoring.

### 1b. Who uses ZA2 (Austria vs Germany)

A2, also covering values and orientation) as proof. A school-leaving certificate and some residence titles count as exemptions. **ÖSD is not mentioned** [S11]. A plain ZA2 certificate therefore does **not** appear to satisfy Modul 1.

## 2. Hören (ca. 15 min · 30 points · 14 items)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Two different texts give the **same information**. Mark the 4 pieces of information you hear | Short radio-style reports (ÖSD: "Radiomeldungen"); model set: a weekend weather report, read by a woman and then a man, with a radio jingle between them | **2 texts of 66–73 words, ~25 s each, 161–170 wpm** (measured 2026-10-24) | **4** · pick 4 of **8** keyword options on one topic sheet. 4 correct → 10, 3 → 7, 2 → 4, 1 → 1. Five marks: −3. More than five: 0 | **once** (each text) | 30 s to read first | S1, S3 |
| Aufgabe 2 | Write the key information from a message onto a note sheet | Voice message / Sprachnachricht (monologue): an informal call from a classmate (du, "grüß dich", fillers "äh"), opened by a playback sound | **1 message, ~124 words, 40.5 s, ~184 wpm** (measured 2026-10-24) | **5** · gap fill on a "Notizen" form (dates, items, € amounts, times, phone number). 5 → 10, linear by 2 | **twice** | 30 s to read first | S1, S3 |
| Aufgabe 3 | Five people are interviewed on one question; tick each person's answers in a grid | Interview with 5 people (vox pop). The interviewer asks once; the answers follow with ~4 s gaps and **no "Text n" labels**; one speaker is a schoolboy | **5 answers of 36–67 words, 12–23 s, 149–191 wpm** (measured 2026-10-24) | **5 rows** · matrix 5 rows × 5 options, **several answers per person possible**. Per row: all correct → 2, one wrong extra tick → 1, more than one wrong / all / none ticked → 0. Max 10 | **once** | 30 s to read first | S1, S3 |

- Track lengths in the 2026 Modellsatz, including instructions, pauses and the repeat: ≈ 2:35 / 3:22 / 4:25 [S1].
- ÖSD exams use speakers from Austria, Germany and Switzerland, each in their standard variety (stated in the ZA1 spec). ZA2 tests "standard spoken listening texts from Austria, Germany and Switzerland". [S3]
- The Durchführungsbestimmungen confirm **14 Hören items** in total. [S2]

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** "Read Aufgabe N carefully, you have 30 seconds." Then a "Situation:" line ("you hear two texts with the same content / a message / an interview with five people"). Then how to answer ("mark the answers; there are four correct ones" / "write the key information on the note sheet" / "several answers per person are possible"). Then the play count.

**TestGerman drift (5 "ÖSD A2" mocks):** the task types are close (8 options with 4 correct; a 5-gap voicemail played twice; a multi-select interview grid). But they weight Hören at 15 points instead of 30. `mock-exams.md` lists Teil 3 as "choose from 5". The mock itself allows multiple answers per person, which matches the real exam. Full comparison (incl. one TTS voice reading both Aufgabe 1 texts and 4 gender mismatches in Aufgabe 3): §9.4.

## 3. Lesen (30 min · 25 points · 10 items)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | Find the right heading for each of 5 texts from a list of **10 headings** (A–K) | 5 short newspaper notices (local news, offers, events), each with an origin label ("[aus einer österreichischen Zeitung]"); one notice includes a small **bar chart**. Folder: "einfache Zeitungstexte" | **≈ 35–55 words per text** (corrected 2026-10-24; was "≈ 45–60") | **5** · matching text → heading letter; a letter may be reused without penalty. **3 points per item** (5 → 15) | – | part of 30 min | S1, S3 |
| Aufgabe 2 | Read one longer text, then answer 5 questions | Newspaper feature / report about people (model set: a human-interest story from Austria) | ≈ 200 words (207 incl. headline) | **5** · multiple choice **A/B/C** (sentence stems completed). **2 points per item** (5 → 10) | – | | S1, S3 |

- Two Aufgaben, global and detailed understanding [S3]. 10 items, 25 points in total [S2].

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** "First read the 10 headings and the 5 texts, then write the matching letter above each text; only one solution per text" / "First read the text, then mark A, B or C for each of the 5 items." A worked example (item 0) is printed.

**TestGerman drift:** their part pairing is inconsistent. Mocks 1–2 use **two** headings tasks. Mocks 4–5 use **two** long-text MC tasks. Only mock 3 has the real Aufgabe 1 + Aufgabe 2 combination. They also weight both parts equally (25 points split evenly) instead of 15 + 10.

## 4. Schreiben (30 min · 15 points)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Schreibaufgabe | Reply to a friend's personal email | Input: an informal email (≈ 70–80 words) with **4 questions** (e.g. about travel, news, meeting up) | **ca. 50 words** | 1 text · answer all 4 questions + closing greeting. The answer sheet prints the reply header and an opening line | – | 30 min | S1, S3 |

**Rating criteria** (Auswertungsbogen Schriftlich Vers. 4.0, 17 Sep 2026) [S5]:

- **Textlänge:** ≥ 40 words, no deduction · 39–36, −1 · 35–31, −2 · 30–26, −3 · < 26 words, task not passed (0)
- **Aufgabe (content):**
  - all 4 questions answered with **extra independent content**: **+1**
  - all answered fully: 0
  - 3 answered fully and 1 short or missing: −1
  - 2 answered fully and 2 only quoted or missing: −2
  - < 50% covered, or mostly quoting the prompt: 0 for the whole task
- **Kommunikative Angemessenheit / Textsorte** (salutation, greeting formula, reference to the input email, register, tone): 2 / 1 / 0
- **Textaufbau / Kohärenz** (connectors like "und", "aber", "weil"; varied sentence openings): 2 / 1 / 0
- **Lexik:** 5 / 4–3 / 2–1 / 0
- **Formale Richtigkeit** (morphology, syntax, spelling): 5 / 4–3 / 2–1 / 0
- Maximum 15. Deductions never take the result below 0. Minimum to pass the subtest: **3**.

**Instruction style (paraphrased):** "Situation: you receive this email from your friend …". Then bullets: write about 50 words, answer all questions (listed), write a greeting at the end. The second sheet says "continue the email and answer the questions".

**TestGerman drift:** they use 7 AI content points and 30 points of weight (real: 4 questions, 15 points). Their 750-character cap is fine for 50 words.

## 5. Sprechen (ca. 10 min · 20 points · pair or individual)

| Teil | Task | Text type / situation | Length | Items · answer format | Plays | Time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1: Sich vorstellen | Pick **5 of 6 topics** on a sheet (Folienblatt) and say a few sentences about each. The conversation partner also asks questions | 6 topic prompts (job, languages, music, travel, family, "… mache gern …") | "a few sentences" per topic | Mostly monologue + follow-up questions | – | within ca. 10 min | S1, S2 |
| Aufgabe 2: Gemeinsam eine Aufgabe lösen | **Plan an activity together** (model set: a class outing), using a prompt sheet with question keywords | Prompt sheet titled with the activity, 4 rows of **picture prompts** (corrected 2026-10-24): Wohin? (3 photos, each with a caption: countryside / a city / a lake) · Wann? (weekday strip, month grid, clock) · Wie reisen? (train, bus, bicycles) · Was mitnehmen? (food, a guitar) + a "noch etwas?" box. 11 pictures in all | – | Dialogue: make suggestions, react, agree/disagree, offer alternatives | – | | S1, S2, S6 |

**Preparation:** 10 minutes alone. Keyword notes may be used during the exam. [S2]

**Opening:** about 1 minute of greeting, examiners introduce themselves, ID check, short **unscored** small talk. [S2]

### Who does what (for our TTS simulation)

- **Two examiners** [S2]:
  - **Assessor/-in:** leads and explains each task.
  - **Interlokutor/-in:** asks **additional questions after Aufgabe 1**. In an **individual exam** they are also **the partner in Aufgabe 2**.
- **Pair exam:** Aufgabe 2 is between the two candidates. Both examiners observe. Relatives or people with the same surname may not be paired. [S2]
- Rated independently by both examiners, then agreed. The exam is recorded. [S2]

For us this means:

1. After the candidate's Aufgabe 1 monologue, a TTS Interlokutor asks 1–2 follow-up questions.
2. In Aufgabe 2, a TTS partner (the individual-exam Interlokutor, or a peer voice for the pair version) makes suggestions, reacts to the candidate's, disagrees at least once and reaches a joint plan across all prompt rows.
3. An "Assessor" voice reads the task explanations.

**Speaking rating criteria** (Auswertungsbogen Mündlich Vers. 4.0, 17 Sep 2026) [S6]:

| Criterion | Max |
|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1: task fulfilment and text production (adequate on 5 topics, mostly monologue, coherent) | 3 |
| Aufgabe 2: task fulfilment, communicative (exchange information, suggest/accept/reject/offer alternatives, politeness) | 3 |
| Aufgaben 1+2: Ausdruck, Wortschatz | 5 |
| Aufgaben 1+2: Verständlichkeit, Aussprache/Intonation, Flüssigkeit | 4 |
| Aufgaben 1+2: Formale Richtigkeit | 5 |
| **Total** | **20** (min. 10) |

**TestGerman drift:** they use 13 solo prompts and 20 min of talk time. Their Aufgabe 2 partner says *Sie* and repeats some lines verbatim (mocks 1 and 5). Their Aufgabe 1 is cut into 6 separate 2-minute question pairs; the real task is one turn choosing 5 of 6 topics. Their Aufgabe 2 plays 7 scripted partner lines turn by turn. The planning genre is right, but there is no reaction to the learner and no prompt sheet.

## 6. Scoring and pass rule

Official Gesamtbogen Vers. 3.0 (5 Jun 2026) [S4] and Durchführungsbestimmungen §6 [S2]:

| Module | Max | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 25 | min. 5 |
| Hören | 30 | min. 6 |
| Schreiben | 15 | min. 3 |
| **Written module** | **70** | **min. 35 (50%)**, and every subtest minimum met |
| **Oral module (Sprechen)** | **20** | **min. 10 (50%)** |
| Overall (optional) | 90 | min. 45, and all minimums met |

- **Grades:**
  - Written: 70–62 sehr gut, 61–50 gut, 49–35 bestanden, 34–0 nicht bestanden
  - Oral: 20–18 sehr gut, 17–15 gut, 14–10 bestanden, 9–0 nicht bestanden
  - Overall: 90–80 sehr gut, 79–64 gut, 63–45 bestanden, 44–0 nicht bestanden [S4][S2]
- The TestGerman label "50% per section" is wrong. It is 50% per **module**, with low subtest floors (20% in each of Lesen and Hören, 3/15 in Schreiben).

## 7. Kurztest composition (proposal)

This mirrors the DTZ cut in `lib/exam/blueprint.ts`: ~10 items per closed skill, every Teil represented in real proportion.

**Lesen: 10 items (real 5 / 5 = 10, so the Kurztest is the full Lesen section)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | 1 | 5 | 5 short texts + 10 headings. Cutting it would break the 2:1 distractor ratio |
| Aufgabe 2 | 1 | 5 | 1 text ≈ 200 words, 5 × A/B/C |

To shorten it anyway: 3 texts + 6 headings (keeps 2:1) and 3 MC items = 6 items. The 3-vs-2-point weighting stays.

**Hören: 10 items (real 4 / 5 / 5 = 14)**

| Teil | Blocks | Items | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aufgabe 1 | 1 | 4 | Kept whole: 2 parallel texts, 8 options, 4 correct (the scoring depends on the 4-of-8 rule) |
| Aufgabe 2 | 1 | 3 | Voice message with 3 note gaps, **played twice** |
| Aufgabe 3 | 1 | 3 | 3 interviewees × 5-option grid, multi-select, played once |

**Schreiben:** the full task (friend's email with 4 questions, ~50 words), rated on the S5 criteria including the length deductions.

**Sprechen:** Aufgabe 1 (5 of 6 topics + 1–2 TTS follow-ups) and Aufgabe 2 (joint planning with a TTS partner over the prompt sheet). Include a short preparation step.

### 9.3 Generation checklist (set 1)

- **Lesen A1:** 10 headings A–I, K in 3 theme clusters (4/3/3); 5 texts of 35–55 words with origin labels (≈ 2 D, 2 A, 1 CH); one text with a small chart or table; each text has 1–3 same-theme near-miss headings.
- **Lesen A2:** ~200-word human-interest story, AT-labelled; example 0 + 5 sentence completions in text order; key skewed toward one letter, as in the MS (4 × A).
- **Hören A1:** two radio texts (f then m) with the same 4 facts in different wording and order, ~65–75 words each, jingle between; 8 boxes in 2 rows; 1 distractor mentioned-and-negated, 3 unmentioned.
- **Hören A2:** informal du voicemail, ~120 words at ~170–185 wpm with fillers; note title + 5 lines; one time stated so that 2 formats are accepted (19 / 7).
- **Hören A3:** interviewer (f) asks once; 5 answers (f, m, f, m, f) of 35–65 words, ~4 s apart, no labels; 10 ticks of 25, each column twice; each speaker names one option they don't do.
- **Track:** previews 30 / 30 / 30 s, check times 20 s and 60 s, gongs, jingle, playback sound; ~10.5 min.
- **Schreiben:** input email ~75 words, first question "Wie geht es dir?", last "Wann hast du Zeit?"-type; reply sheet with "AW:", salutation, 2 opening sentences, "Zu deinen Fragen:".
- **Sprechen:** Folienblatt with 6 keywords incl. one open "… …" item; planning sheet with title + 4 picture rows (3 + 3 + 3 + 2 pictures) + "noch etwas?"; Interlokutor as active du partner (individual) or a peer (pair).
