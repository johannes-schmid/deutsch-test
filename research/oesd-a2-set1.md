### 2.3 Schreiben (30 min)

Input email (~75 w, A) from your friend **Jana** in Klagenfurt, subject "Neue Arbeit": she has started work in a kindergarten, likes the children, is tired in the evenings, and would love a visit. Questions in the body and as bullets:
**Wie geht es dir? · Was machst du gerade beruflich? · Was hast du am letzten Wochenende gemacht? · Wann kannst du mich besuchen?**
Reply sheet: "AW: Neue Arbeit", "Liebe Jana," + "vielen Dank für dein E-Mail! Schön, dass dir die Arbeit gefällt. Hier meine Antworten:". Instruction "Schreiben Sie ein E-Mail an Jana", circa 50 words, all questions, greeting at the end. Scoring: `oesd-a2.md` §4.

## 3. What has to exist before drafting

1. **`lib/exam/exams/oesd-a2.ts` `full` spec** with the corrected numbers (L1 35–55 w; H1 65–75 w per text; H2 ~120 w; H3 35–65 w; ~175 wpm) and the measured track rhythm. The file still has the old numbers.
2. **Pipeline:** `research/oesd-a2-authoring.md`, `scripts/modelltest/plan-oesd-a2.ts` (one entry per set across all skills), `clash.ts` rules.
3. **Components** (design skills first; each on `/design/pruefung`):
   - heading matching: 10 headings A–I, K → 5 texts with origin labels, one text with a chart/table image, letters reusable
   - A/B/C sentence completion with a printed example (`ChoiceItem` exists; example rendering to check)
   - "mark exactly 4 of 8" boxes in 2 rows, with the 5-marks −3 and > 5 = 0 rules
   - note sheet with title and 5 lines (`GapItem` "Notiz" exists)
   - multi-select grid 5 × 5 with 2/1/0 per row and "all or none ticked = 0"
   - email reply sheet with "AW:" header and a printed opener
   - Sprechen: Folienblatt (choose 5 of 6), picture planning sheet (4 rows + "noch etwas?"), live Interlokutor as du partner
   - result view per module with subtest minima and Prädikat
4. **Scoring helpers:** L1 3 pts/item, L2 2 pts/item; H1 10/7/4/1/0 with penalties; H2 10/8/6/4/2/0 with exact phone; H3 per row; the Schreiben rubric (TL, A ± incl. +1, K 2, T 2, L 5, F 5, n. b.); the Sprechen rubric (3/3/5/4/5).
5. **Assets:** 11 small pictures for the planning sheet (Pexels/pictograms), one simple table for L1 text 3, a radio jingle, the voicemail playback sound, the gong.
